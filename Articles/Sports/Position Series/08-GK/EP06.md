# SERIES 08 — The Evolution of the Goalkeeper

# EP06 — SPECIAL: Who Is the Best Goalkeeper in Modern Football?

## Courtois, Raya and the Goalkeepers Chasing Them

30 May 2026, Budapest. Champions League final, PSG vs Arsenal.

The penalty shootout.

Arsenal goalkeeper **David Raya** saved Nuno Mendes' kick.

But Arsenal missed twice, and PSG lifted the trophy.

That final did not complete Raya's season.

But it does not define it either.

Because just eleven days earlier, Raya had helped Arsenal become Premier League champions for the first time in 22 years.

19 clean sheets. A third consecutive Golden Glove.

In Madrid that same season, a different story was unfolding.

Real Madrid changed their coach and failed to win a single trophy.

But amid the chaos, one goalkeeper did not collapse:

**Thibaut Courtois.**

When the ten nominees for the 2026 Yashin Trophy were announced, many experts named two goalkeepers among the leading favourites:

**Courtois and Raya.**

*(The 2026 Yashin Trophy will be announced at the Ballon d'Or ceremony in London on 26 October.)*

---

# The Criteria

We use the five criteria from this series.

**Shot-Stopping** — the ability to make saves

**Sweeping** — range in defending the space behind the line

**Distribution** — build-up and passing

**Command** — control of the box, aerial balls and defensive organisation

**Big Moments** — influence in decisive matches

*(The star ratings are this article's subjective assessment.)*

---

# 1. Thibaut Courtois

## THE WALL

```text
Shot-Stopping  ★★★★★
Sweeping       ★★★
Distribution   ★★★
Command        ★★★★★
Big Moments    ★★★★★
```

Courtois is **the best shot-stopper** of this era.

Nearly two metres tall, with enormous reach — and a reaction speed that does not seem to fit his frame.

His career is full of big-game moments.

**2018 World Cup** — Golden Glove, with Belgium finishing third.

**2022 Champions League final** — nine saves against Liverpool, the most in a Champions League final since 2003-04 according to Opta. He won the Yashin Trophy the same year.

**2024 Champions League final** — back from an ACL injury, he kept a clean sheet against Dortmund and won the trophy again.

Then came 2025-26.

Real Madrid were falling apart.

Xabi Alonso was dismissed in January, they were knocked out of the Champions League by Bayern in the quarter-finals, and they finished a distant second to Barcelona in the league.

But Courtois kept **17 clean sheets in 45 appearances.**

In a 1-0 defeat at Anfield, he made eight saves, including a point-blank stop from Szoboszlai.

Against Manchester City, he denied Haaland and Cherki.

At the 2026 World Cup, he played every one of Belgium's matches and led them to the quarter-finals.

In the round of 16, Belgium beat the USA 4-1, and during the tournament he became Belgium's record World Cup appearance-maker.

Belgium lost to eventual champions Spain in the quarter-final, but Courtois was Belgium's strongest player to the very end.

Courtois' weakness is his footwork.

He is not a goalkeeper who leads the build-up like Ederson or Raya.

But remember the conclusion of EP05:

**The team can help with a build-up weakness. Nobody can help with a saving weakness.**

# WHEN THE TEAM FALLS, HE DOES NOT

# 2. David Raya

## THE COMPLETE MODERN GOALKEEPER

```text
Shot-Stopping  ★★★★★
Sweeping       ★★★★
Distribution   ★★★★★
Command        ★★★★
Big Moments    ★★★★
```

Raya brings together every theme of this series in one player.

In 2023, Arteta chose him over Ramsdale.

The reason was build-up.

Many people had doubts:

**“Is it right to change your first-choice goalkeeper for the sake of build-up?”**

Three years later, the answer is clear.

```text
2023-24  GOLDEN GLOVE
2024-25  GOLDEN GLOVE
2025-26  GOLDEN GLOVE (19 CLEAN SHEETS IN 37 GAMES) + PREMIER LEAGUE TITLE
```

Three consecutive Golden Gloves is a rare achievement in Premier League history.

His 19 clean sheets also matched David Seaman's club record for a Premier League season.

And Raya was not simply a ball-playing goalkeeper.

Against Chelsea in March 2026, with Arsenal leading 2-1 late in the game, he stretched to his limit to keep out what would have been the equaliser.

Arteta said afterwards that his **“heart almost stopped.”**

In the first leg of the Champions League quarter-final against Sporting, he made five saves to protect a 1-0 win.

After that match, Havertz called Raya **“the best goalkeeper in the world.”**

Raya's greatest value is that **he can do both.**

He starts Arsenal's attacks with precise long passes under pressure,

and he stops goals at the decisive moments.

And Raya still has unfinished business.

At the 2026 World Cup, he was Spain's second-choice goalkeeper.

Spain's title and the Golden Glove belonged to their No.1, Unai Simón.

# SAVES + FEET + TITLES

# 3. Joint Number One — Why Two?

Courtois and Raya are the best in different ways.

```text
                     COURTOIS               RAYA
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GREATEST WEAPON      SHOT-STOPPING          SAVES + BUILD-UP
TEAM CONTEXT         HOLDS A FALLING TEAM   COMPLETES A WINNING TEAM
2025-26              17 CS IN 45 GAMES      19 LEAGUE CS IN 37 GAMES
TROPHIES (25-26)     —                      PREMIER LEAGUE
BIGGEST STAGE        2 CL FINAL WINS        CL FINAL RUNNER-UP
INTERNATIONAL        WC QUARTER-FINAL (No.1) WC WINNER (No.2)
```

**Courtois** is the best shot-stopper in the line of EP02's goalkeepers — **the heir to Buffon and Casillas.**

**Raya** is the goalkeeper of EP04 — **the line that came after Neuer and Ederson** — who has also mastered shot-stopping.

To the question this whole series has asked, they give two different answers:

**“Saves or feet?”**

Courtois proves that **shot-stopping is still the foundation.**

Raya proves that **if you can do both, that is best of all.**

# TWO PATHS, TWO ANSWERS

# 4. The Goalkeepers Chasing Them

### Joan García — THE NEXT ONE

```text
Shot-Stopping  ★★★★★
Distribution   ★★★★
```

Joan García moved from Espanyol to Barcelona in the summer of 2025, won La Liga's Golden Glove in his first season, and Barcelona won the league.

He is also among the leading favourites for the 2026 Yashin Trophy, alongside Courtois and Raya.

He was Spain's third-choice goalkeeper at the World Cup, but he is very likely to become the next owner of Spain's goal.

### Matvey Safonov — THE SURPRISE CHAMPION

```text
Shot-Stopping  ★★★★★
Distribution   ★★★
```

PSG's back-up goalkeeper produced the most dramatic twist of the 2025-26 season.

Shootout saves in the Intercontinental Cup in December, a start in place of Chevalier in January, and the No.1 shirt in the second half of the season.

He stood out at Anfield in the Champions League quarter-final against Liverpool, and kept goal in the final as PSG won back-to-back titles.

He is the clearest example of the lesson from EP05: **footwork cannot replace shot-stopping.**

### Unai Simón — THE WORLD CHAMPION

At the 2026 World Cup, Spain beat Argentina 1-0 in the final.

Simón kept seven clean sheets in eight matches, conceding just once, and won the Golden Glove.

That he finished the tournament as Spain's No.1, with Raya and Joan García behind him, says everything about his value.

### Gianluigi Donnarumma

The 2025 Yashin Trophy winner.

In his first season at City, he kept 15 clean sheets and finished second in the Golden Glove race.

But he was not among the nominees for the 2026 Yashin Trophy.

### Alisson Becker

The first-ever Yashin Trophy winner in 2019, and one of the most complete goalkeepers of this era.

But in 2025-26, a hamstring injury kept him out during an important stretch of the season.

At his peak, he remains among the very best — but if the question is who is best right now, Courtois and Raya are ahead.

### Emiliano Martínez

The save from Kolo Muani in the 2022 World Cup final, and back-to-back Yashin Trophies in 2023 and 2024.

He is among the nominees for the 2026 Yashin Trophy once again.

# 6. The Right Goalkeeper for the Situation

The best goalkeeper depends on the situation.

**A one-off Champions League final?**

→ Courtois. His two finals prove it.

**A title race across a whole season?**

→ Raya. Three consecutive Golden Gloves and a league title.

**A team that is starting to fall apart?**

→ Courtois.

**A team with a high line and build-up from the back?**

→ Raya.

**A penalty shootout?**

→ Safonov or Martínez.

# THE BEST GOALKEEPER DEPENDS ON THE QUESTION

# 7. Verdict — The Best Goalkeepers Right Now

Let us recall the conclusion of this series:

**Shot-stopping is the foundation. Footwork is the extension.**

By that standard:

# COURTOIS — THE BEST SHOT-STOPPER

# RAYA — THE BEST COMPLETE GOALKEEPER

Courtois is **the most decisive goalkeeper.**

Raya is **the goalkeeper who best combines everything modern football demands.**

And right now, the two of them are the best goalkeepers in the world.

# Conclusion — The Position That Changed the Most, and the Least

In SERIES 08, we have looked at 30 years of the goalkeeper position.

The back-pass rule, Neuer's sweeping, Ederson's passing, Donnarumma's departure, Safonov's twist, Raya's title.

The goalkeeper is one of the positions that has changed the most.

But it is also the position that has changed the least.

In 1990, and in 2026,

**the most important moment for a goalkeeper is still the moment the opponent's shot flies towards his goal.**

> **The goalkeeper's job has grown. But the reason the goalkeeper exists has not changed.**
