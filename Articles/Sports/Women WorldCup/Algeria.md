## Les Fennecs

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Algeria play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

**One-line verdict:** A team built largely from **French-born diaspora players** (Lyon, Paris FC, Nice, Lens, Lille, plus the WSL and NWSL), coached by a former PSG/Lyon manager. Organised and dangerous from set pieces and penalties — but **beaten by physical, fast teams** (Malawi 1-3, South Africa 0-1 and 0-2), and inexperienced at this level.

## 5. Head Coach — Farid Benstiti

- **Former PSG and Lyon women's coach** — brings French top-level methods.
- Built the squad around **French-born dual nationals**, many from France's D1/D2.
- Took Algeria from qualification wins over Cameroon to a **best-ever WAFCON third place** and the World Cup.

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Five Games

### Game ① 23 & 28 October 2025 — Algeria 2-1, 1-0 Cameroon
- Dafeur scored in both legs; clean sheet away.
**Lesson:** Algeria can beat a traditional African power with organisation and set plays.

### Game ② 30 July 2026, WAFCON — Morocco 1-0 Algeria
- Mssoudy 84'. **Lesson:** close games against compact teams — decided late.

### Game ③ 8 August 2026, WAFCON QF — Ivory Coast 1-2 Algeria
- Khiri 56', Ould Braham 64'. **Lesson:** strong second halves.

### Game ④ 12 August 2026, WAFCON SF — Algeria 1-3 Malawi
- **Belkhiter red card (first half)**; Tabitha Chawinga scored twice (one a bicycle kick), Temwa once; Adjabi 60'.
**Lesson:** **Fast, physical, individually brilliant forwards** overwhelm Algeria's back line — and indiscipline hurt them.

### Game ⑤ 14 & 17 April 2026 — South Africa 1-0, 2-0 Algeria
**Lesson:** a powerful, athletic team held them scoreless for 180 minutes.

## 11. Weaknesses — With Evidence

1. **Pace and power against them** — Malawi 1-3; South Africa 0-1, 0-2.
2. **No elite player** — top rating 77.
3. **Discipline** — Belkhiter's first-half red card in the semi-final.
4. **Inexperience** — first World Cup; no games against non-African top-30 teams in 2025-26.
5. **Light midfield** — Bethi PHY 51.

---

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Use pace and 1v1 forwards** — Malawi's Chawingas scored three.
2. **Physical pressure in midfield** — target Bethi (PHY 51).
3. **Score early** — Algeria's goals mostly come in the second half and late; an early deficit takes them out of their plan.
4. **Defend set pieces and avoid fouls near the box** — Dafeur penalties.
5. **Stay switched on after 80'** — Algeria score late (83', 87', 88', 90+2').

```text
PACE + 1v1 FORWARDS
       +
PHYSICAL MIDFIELD PRESSURE
       +
EARLY GOAL
       +
LATE CONCENTRATION
       ↓
ALGERIA'S ORGANISATION CANNOT MATCH ELITE ATHLETICISM
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

Algeria are well-coached, organised and on the rise, with a French-trained diaspora core. But they have no star, little experience at this level, and they were beaten by the fastest and most powerful teams they faced.

> **Run at Algeria, press their playmaker, and stay alert to the end.**
