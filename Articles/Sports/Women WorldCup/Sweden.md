# Sweden — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## Blågult

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> ⚠️ **Qualification status:** not yet qualified. Sweden finished **third in League A Group 1** (behind Denmark and Italy) and entered the UEFA play-offs. They beat **Lithuania 5-0 and 10-0** in round 1 (October 2026). Round 2 is against **Scotland (26 November / 5 December 2026 per UEFA; Sweden's federation lists 1 and 5 December)**; the winner qualifies.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Sweden play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

**One-line verdict:** Sweden still have the set-piece power and athletic profile that won World Cup bronze twice. But under new coach Tony Gustavsson they have **won one of eight games against top-15 opponents**, **lost twice to Denmark**, and conceded **five of 15 goals in stoppage time** (45+3', 45', 90+2', 90+4', 90+6'). A team in transition, losing its captain to injury and short of creativity in midfield.

## 5. Head Coach — Tony Gustavsson

- Appointed 1 August 2025, succeeding Peter Gerhardsson (2017–2025; two World Cup bronzes, Olympic silver).
- **Background:** assistant to Pia Sundhage and Jill Ellis with the USA (2012 Olympic gold, 2015 and 2019 World Cup titles); head coach of **Australia 2020–2024** (2023 World Cup semi-final).
- **Known traits from Australia:**
  - **Little rotation in tournaments** — at the 2023 World Cup "Gustavsson rarely turned to his bench", and a tired Australia lost the bronze match 0-2 to Sweden.
  - **Defensive set-ups against stronger teams** (a defensive-minded system at the 2024 Olympics, with Mary Fowler moved to lone striker).
  - Criticised for "his tactical decisions and substitution patterns".
- **Record with Sweden so far:** W 4 D 3 L 5; W 1 D 2 L 5 against top-15 teams.

### 6.2 Build-Up
- Direct: centre-backs play long to **Blackstenius** (PHY 76) or into the channels for **Rytting Kaneryd** (PAC 89).
- **Angeldahl** (Real Madrid) is the main passer from midfield (PAS 79); **Zigiotti Olme** sits deeper.
- Without **Asllani** (injured, PAS 81), Sweden lack a creator between the lines.

### 6.3 Attacking Entry
1. **Crosses and set pieces** — the traditional Swedish weapon (Ilestedt scored twice vs Lithuania; Ilestedt and Blackstenius are aerial threats).
2. **Rytting Kaneryd's pace on the right** — PAC 89; scored vs Denmark (52').
3. **Rolfö inside from the left** — the most complete player (all six stats 81–82); scored vs Italy (73').
4. **Blackstenius in behind** — 132 caps, 48 goals; PAC 84.
5. **Late substitutes** — Ijeh and Kafaji scored at 84' and 90+2' vs France.

### 6.4 Set Pieces
- **Ilestedt** (CB, 84 caps, **15 goals**) — one of the most dangerous set-piece defenders in Europe.
- **Björn**, **Eriksson**, **Blackstenius** — tall targets.
- In 2023, Sweden scored much of their World Cup total from set plays (Ilestedt scored four).

### 6.5 Game-State Behaviour

| State | What Sweden do | Evidence |

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

### Strategy 1: Run in behind the slow centre-backs
- **Evidence:** Harder (Denmark) scored in both qualifiers; Eriksson PAC 54, Ilestedt PAC 63.
- **How:** a quick striker who runs diagonally between the CBs; through balls from a No. 10; fast wingers attack inside the full-backs.

### Strategy 2: Keep the ball and press their midfield
- **Evidence:** Spain 4-0 and 1-0 (5-0 on aggregate).
- **How:** possession through the thirds; press Zigiotti Olme when she receives — Sweden then go long.

### Strategy 3: Attack at the end of each half
- **Evidence:** five of 15 goals conceded in stoppage time; four more between 30' and 36'.
- **How:** keep the tempo high in the last 10 minutes of each half; make an attacking substitution around 75'.

### Strategy 4: Defend set pieces with your tallest players and avoid fouls
- **Evidence:** Ilestedt 15 goals; Sweden's World Cup 2023 run was built on set plays.
- **How:** zonal marking with a strong first-ball defender; no cheap fouls in your half.

### Strategy 5: Don't drop deep after 70'
- **Evidence:** Italy 0-2 → 2-2 (70', 73'); France 0-1 → 2-1 (84', 90+2'); Sweden scored 7 of 8 goals vs top teams in the second half.
- **How:** keep the ball, keep the defensive line high enough to win second balls, bring on fresh defenders.

### Strategy 6: Shoot early and cross into the six-yard box
- **Evidence:** Falk OVR 79 (SPD 28).
- **How:** shots from distance, low crosses across the goal.

```text
RUN IN BEHIND (Eriksson PAC 54, Ilestedt PAC 63)
          +
KEEP THE BALL, PRESS THE No. 6
          +
ATTACK AT 40'–45' AND 85'–90+'
          +
NO CHEAP SET PIECES
          +
DON'T SIT DEEP AFTER 70'
          ↓
DENMARK 2-1 ×2 — THE BLUEPRINT
```

## 13. Conclusion

Sweden remain a physical, experienced tournament team with two World Cup bronzes and an elite set-piece threat. But 2025-26 has been their worst run in years: **one win in eight games against top-15 opponents**, **two defeats to Denmark**, and a defence that keeps **conceding at the end of halves**.

The new coach has not yet solved the absence of Asllani's creativity, and the centre-backs are among the slowest in the tournament.

> **Run at Sweden's centre-backs, keep the ball, and attack hardest when the clock runs down.**
