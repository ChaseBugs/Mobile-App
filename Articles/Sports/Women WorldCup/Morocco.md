# Morocco — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## Atlas Lionesses

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Morocco play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

```text
QUALIFICATION       WAFCON 2026 quarter-final winners (8 August 2026, 2-1 vs South Africa, as hosts)

```

**One-line verdict:** A well-organised, defensively solid host-nation side from the 2026 WAFCON, coached by the man who won the 2023 World Cup with Spain. They reached the 2023 World Cup knockout rounds. But **they barely score in big games** (three goals in their last four games at the 2026 WAFCON), and **they lose penalty shootouts and big leads.**

## 5. Head Coach — Jorge Vilda

- **Won the 2023 World Cup with Spain**; now Morocco's head coach.
- Brings **positional, possession-based principles** and strong defensive organisation.
- Under Vilda, Morocco were **unbeaten in normal time** at the home WAFCON — but scored only three goals in their last four games (Algeria, Senegal, South Africa, Cameroon).

### 6.2 Defensive Solidity
- Clean sheets vs Senegal, Cameroon (120'), New Zealand.
- vs South Africa: built a two-goal lead, then "defended resolutely."

### 6.3 Attacking Threat
- **Ibtissam Jraïdi** (Al-Ahli) — the record scorer; goals vs Kenya, Benin, Mali, Haiti.
- **Sanaa Mssoudy** — **84' winner vs Algeria**, braces vs Burkina Faso and Tanzania, 90+4' vs Burkina Faso.
- **Sakina Ouzraoui** — penalties and 31' vs South Africa.

### 6.4 Penalties
- **For:** Ouzraoui and Aït El Haj convert in normal time.
- **Shootouts:** lost two in four days at the 2026 WAFCON (1-3 vs Cameroon, 2-3 vs Algeria). In 2023, Chebbak's World Cup penalty vs Colombia was saved (Lahmari scored the rebound).

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Six Games

### Game ① 2023 World Cup — Morocco 1-0 Korea Republic
- Jraïdi's goal; compact defending. **Lesson:** Morocco win low-scoring games with one moment.

### Game ② 3 August 2023, World Cup — Morocco 1-0 Colombia (Perth)
- Chebbak's penalty was saved; **Lahmari scored the rebound** in first-half stoppage time; Morocco held on → **Germany eliminated.**
**Lesson:** organised and resilient against a much stronger attack.

### Game ③ 2023 World Cup — Germany 6-0 Morocco; R16 France 4-0 Morocco
- Diani headed in at 15'; Dali and Le Sommer followed quickly.
**Lesson:** **Elite European teams overwhelm them** — especially from crosses and set pieces.

### Game ④ 2025, WAFCON 2024 Final — Morocco 2-3 Nigeria
- Led **2-0** at home, lost 2-3.
**Lesson:** **cannot protect leads** against powerful, direct teams.

### Game ⑤ 8 August 2026, WAFCON QF — Morocco 2-1 South Africa
- Ouzraoui 31', Aït El Haj pen 51', then defended deep.
**Lesson:** the classic Vilda pattern — score, then **sit on the lead.**

### Game ⑥ 12 & 15 August 2026 — Cameroon 0-0 (L pens), Algeria 1-1 (L pens)
- 240 minutes, one goal, two shootout defeats at home.
**Lesson:** **no cutting edge** in knockout games — and a **shootout problem.**

## 11. Weaknesses — With Evidence

1. **Low scoring in big games** — 1-0, 0-0, 2-1, 0-0, 1-1 at the 2026 WAFCON after the opener.
2. **Penalty shootouts** — lost two in four days (2026); Chebbak's 2023 penalty saved.
3. **Losing leads** — 2-0 up in the 2024 WAFCON final, lost 2-3.
4. **Heavy defeats to elite Europeans** — Germany 0-6, France 0-4 (2023).
5. **Crosses and headers against** — Diani's header (France); Germany scored many from crosses.
6. **Lack of top-league players** — few in EA-licensed or top-five European leagues.

---

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Score first.** Morocco rarely come back.
2. **Attack with crosses and set pieces** — France and Germany scored freely in the air.
3. **Be patient against the block** — Morocco defend well in a compact 4-5-1; circulate and switch play.
4. **If they score, push** — they drop deep and have lost a 2-0 lead.
5. **Take it to penalties if needed** — they lost two shootouts in four days at home.
6. **Mark Jraïdi and Chebbak** — their main creative and finishing threats.

```text
SCORE FIRST
      +
CROSSES AND SET PIECES
      +
PATIENT CIRCULATION AGAINST THE BLOCK
      +
KEEP PUSHING WHEN THEY LEAD
      ↓
MOROCCO'S LOW-SCORING STYLE CANNOT CHASE GAMES
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

Morocco are organised, disciplined and coached by a World Cup winner. They can beat mid-level teams 1-0 and reach knockout rounds. But they score very little, lose shootouts and leads, and were heavily beaten by Europe's best in 2023.

> **Score first, attack them in the air, and Morocco's caution becomes their trap.**
