## La Albiceleste

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.

> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Argentina play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Five Games Against Strong Opponents

### Game ① 2019 World Cup — Argentina 0-0 Japan
- Held Japan with a deep block. **Lesson:** Argentina can frustrate technical teams.

### Game ② 2019 World Cup — Scotland 3-3 Argentina
- Came back from 0-3 in the last 20 minutes. **Lesson:** never consider them beaten.

### Game ③ 7 March 2026, SheBelieves — Canada 0-0 Argentina (Canada won on pens)
- Clean sheet against a physical, top-10 team.
**Lesson:** Argentina's block is **hard to break** — but they offer nothing in attack.

## 11. Weaknesses — With Evidence

1. **No goals against good teams** — one goal in five games vs USA, Colombia (×2), Canada before the Brazil friendlies.
2. **Conceding early against fast teams** — Brazil scored three in the first 45 (Porto Alegre) and two by 28' (Recife).
3. **Lack of pace** — Cometti PAC 62; no elite fast forward.
4. **Over-reliance on Bonsegundo.**
5. **No World Cup win ever** (four tournaments).
6. **Shootouts** — lost to Canada 2-3 on penalties.
7. **Low-level club football** — most players in Argentina's domestic league or Spain's second tier.

---

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Score early.** Brazil scored three by half-time. An early goal forces Argentina out of their block.
2. **Press high and use pace.** Argentina's defenders are slow; run in behind Cometti.
3. **Be patient against the block** — work the ball wide, cross, and shoot from the edge of the box.
4. **Do not concede set pieces around the box** — Bonsegundo is their main weapon.
5. **Don't relax when ahead** — Argentina came back from 0-2 vs Brazil (2026), 0-2 vs South Africa (2023), 0-3 vs Scotland (2019).

```text
EARLY GOAL + PACE IN BEHIND
          +
PATIENCE AGAINST THE LOW BLOCK
          +
NO CHEAP FREE KICKS NEAR THE BOX
          +
KEEP THE FOOT DOWN AFTER SCORING
          ↓
ARGENTINA CANNOT CHASE A GAME AGAINST A STRONG TEAM
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

Argentina are organised, physical and resilient — capable of 0-0s against Canada and Colombia, and comebacks against anyone. But they have no elite attacker and have never won a World Cup game.

> **Score first, keep running at them, and give Bonsegundo nothing to aim at.**
