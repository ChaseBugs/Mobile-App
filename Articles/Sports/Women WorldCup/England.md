# England — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## The Lionesses

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> ⚠️ **Qualification status:** not yet qualified. England finished **second in League A Group 3** behind Spain and entered the UEFA play-offs. They beat **Greece 3-1 and 5-0** in round 1 (October 2026). Round 2 is against **Ukraine (26 November away, 5 December at Wembley)**; the winner qualifies. England beat Ukraine 6-1 and 3-0 in the league phase, so qualification is highly likely.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how England play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

**One-line verdict:** A team of **Euro winners at elite clubs** (Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester City/United), with a goalkeeper rated **87** and a striker rated **88**. England are the best tournament team in Europe — they **never lose their nerve**. But against **Spain and Brazil** they were overrun in midfield in two of three games: **0-4 in Mallorca with no shot on goal**, and **0-2 down after 20 minutes** against 11-player Brazil.

## 5. Head Coach — Sarina Wiegman

- Took charge in September 2021 after winning Euro 2017 with the Netherlands.
- **Achievements:** **Euro 2022, Euro 2025, 2023 Finalissima, 2023 World Cup final** — the best tournament coach in women's football.
- **Style:**
  - **Pragmatic:** possession 4-2-3-1 against most teams, **low block** against Spain at Wembley ("sit in a low block and counter" — Walsh).
  - **Tournament management:** her substitutions changed every Euro 2025 knockout game (Agyemang, Kelly, Beever-Jones from the bench).
  - **Mental strength:** England never panic when trailing.
- **Admitted weakness:** "we had hoped to press higher but were pushed back" (Wembley, April 2026) — her team cannot press Spain for 90 minutes.

## 6. Tactics and Formation

### 6.1 Shapes

```text
IN POSSESSION (4-2-3-1 → 2-3-5)

        JAMES / KELLY        RUSSO           HEMP
                        TOONE / PARK
              STANWAY                 WALSH
   BRONZE / CHARLES   WILLIAMSON   LE TISSIER / CARTER   GREENWOOD
                          HAMPTON

OUT OF POSSESSION vs ELITE (4-4-1-1 / 4-5-1 LOW BLOCK)

                          RUSSO
                       STANWAY / TOONE
   HEMP       WALSH      CLINTON / KENDALL       JAMES / PARK
   CHARLES    WILLIAMSON / MORGAN    LE TISSIER    BRONZE
                          HAMPTON
```

### 6.2 Build-Up
- **Hampton** (EA KIC 89) — the best kicking goalkeeper in the tournament; plays long diagonals to Hemp or James.
- **Walsh** (PAS 81) drops between the centre-backs; **Greenwood** (LB, PAS 79) inverts.
- **Le Tissier** (CB, PAC 84) and **Williamson** step into midfield.

### 6.3 Attacking Entry
1. **Hemp 1v1 on the left** — PAC 92, DRI 87. Scored vs Spain (3').
2. **Russo as a target and link** — SHO 88, DRI 86, PHY 78; drops to combine, then attacks the box.
3. **Stanway late runs and long shots** — 11 goals in 12 games.
4. **Lauren James** — the most unpredictable dribbler (PAC 90, DRI 87); two goals vs Greece.
5. **Crosses to the far post** — Kelly (PAS 85), Mead (PAS 82), Bronze.

### 6.4 Pressing and Block
- **Home vs weaker teams:** high 4-4-2 press, Stanway jumping.
- **Against Spain:** compact low block and direct counters — won 1-0 at Wembley, lost 0-4 in Mallorca when the block broke.

### 6.5 Set Pieces and Penalties
- **Stanway** — five penalties in 12 games; an England penalty record.
- **Greenwood** free kicks (hit the bar vs Brazil).
- Corners: Williamson, Le Tissier, Carter (scored vs Ukraine, 14').
- **Shoot-outs:** won the Euro 2025 QF and final, and the 2023 Finalissima.

### 6.6 Game-State Behaviour

| State | What England do | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Leading | Drop into a low block; Hampton keeps them in it | Spain 1-0 (Wembley) |
| Trailing | **Bring on finishers; long balls to Agyemang/Russo; never stop** | Sweden 0-2 → 2-2; Italy 0-1 → 2-1 |
| Trailing against elite possession | **Cannot get the ball** | Spain 0-4: no shots on target |
| Late, level | Very dangerous | 90+6', 119' (Euro 2025 SF) |

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Six Games

### Game ① 14 April 2026 — England 1-0 Spain (Wembley)

| Minute | Event |
|---|---|
| **3'** | **Hemp scrambles it in — goal-line technology: over "by one inch"** |
| ~5'–15' | Hemp hits the post |
| 1st half | Spain one shot on target; Carmona and V. López hit the woodwork |
| 2nd half | Spain double up on Hemp; England in a deep block |
| Late | **Hampton point-blank save from Imade (corner)** |

**How England won:** an early goal, then a **low block** ("sit in a low block and counter" — Walsh), a world-class goalkeeper and some luck (woodwork twice).
**Lesson:** England **can beat Spain without the ball**. But it took an early goal and two shots off the frame.

### Game ② 5 June 2026 — Spain 4-0 England (Mallorca)

| Minute | Event |

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

### Strategy 1: Control midfield and press Walsh
- **Evidence:** Spain 4-0 in Mallorca (no English shot on target).
- **How:** a striker or No. 10 screens Walsh; overload either side of her with your No. 8s; counter-press immediately after losing the ball.

### Strategy 2: Start fast and direct
- **Evidence:** Brazil 2-0 inside 20'; Sweden 2-0 early at Euro 2025.
- **How:** attack in the first 20 minutes, especially behind Bronze/Greenwood.

### Strategy 3: Run at the slow full-backs
- **Evidence:** Bronze PAC 71, Greenwood PAC 72.
- **How:** fastest wingers against them in 1v1; runs in behind from the striker into the channel.

### Strategy 4: Kill the game — a lead is not enough
- **Evidence:** Sweden 2-0 at 79' → lost; Italy 1-0 at 90+5' → lost; Spain 1-0 → lost on penalties.
- **How:** keep the ball rather than dropping deep; make defensive changes before England's attacking substitutes come on; win second balls against Agyemang/Russo; **avoid a shoot-out** (England won three of three recently).

### Strategy 5: Double-mark Hemp and stop Stanway's penalties
- **Evidence:** Spain neutralised Hemp after half-time; Stanway has scored five penalties in 12 games.
- **How:** full-back plus winger on Hemp; no tackles in the box.

### Strategy 6: If you defend, defend deep and compact
- **Evidence:** 10-player Brazil held England to a penalty for 70 minutes.
- **How:** two lines of four close together; England's crossing is less effective against a packed box.

```text
CONTROL MIDFIELD + PRESS WALSH
          +
FAST START (first 20')
          +
SPEED vs BRONZE / GREENWOOD
          +
DOUBLE-MARK HEMP
          +
KEEP ATTACKING WHEN AHEAD — NO PENALTIES
          ↓
SPAIN 4-0: THE ONLY WAY TO BEAT A TEAM THAT NEVER GIVES UP
```

## 13. Conclusion

England are **back-to-back European champions** with an elite spine: **Hampton (87), Williamson, Walsh, Stanway, Russo (88)**. Their tournament mentality is the best in the world. They trailed in all three Euro 2025 knockout games and won all three.

But 2025-26 showed the limits. **Spain beat them 4-0 without allowing a shot on target**, and **Brazil led 2-0 inside 20 minutes**. England can be dominated by a team that **controls midfield, presses Walsh, and attacks their slow full-backs** — as long as that team keeps going after taking the lead.

> **Take England's midfield, take the lead — and never stop attacking until the final whistle.**
