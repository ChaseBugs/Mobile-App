>

**One-line verdict:** A **midfield-led, defensively solid** team that reached the Euro 2025 semi-final and came within seconds of the final. Giugliano (85), Caruso (83) and Greggi (83) give them real technical quality. But in 2025-26 Italy **did not beat a single top-15 opponent in eight attempts**, scored only **four goals** in those games, and drew 1-1 at home with **Belarus**. Italy are hard to beat and **very hard to lose to** — but they struggle badly to score.

## 5. Head Coach — Andrea Soncin

- In charge since 2023 (succeeded Milena Bertolini after the 2023 World Cup group exit).
- **Best result:** Euro 2025 semi-final, Italy's best European finish in decades.
- **Approach:** a compact block, technical midfield, and minimal risk. Italy concede few goals but create little against top opponents.
- **Concern:** zero wins against top-15 teams in 2025-26 friendlies and qualifiers.

### 6.2 Build-Up
- **Giugliano** (Roma, PAS 83, DRI 86) — the playmaker; drops deep to receive.
- **Caruso** (Bayern, DRI 87) and **Greggi** (Roma, DRI 85, PAC 84) carry the ball.
- **Boattin** (LB, PAS 82, DRI 84) — the most creative defender; delivers set pieces.
- Centre-backs Linari (PAC 67) and Salvai (PAC 73) are not progressive passers (PAS 64, 70).

### 6.3 Attacking Entry
1. **Wing-backs** — **Di Guglielmo** (PAC 90) on the right; Boattin/Bergamaschi on the left.
2. **Giugliano's through balls and set pieces.**
3. **Girelli** (132 caps, 62 goals, now 36) — the box finisher; late winner vs Norway (90').
4. **Late runs from Caruso and Greggi** (Caruso 18 goals).

### 6.4 Defence
- Compact low/mid block; **conceded only one goal in two games vs Denmark**.
- **Weak against pace in transition** — the USA scored five in two games.

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

### Strategy 1: Score first — Italy can't come back
- **Evidence:** after conceding first vs Brazil, Sweden and the USA, Italy scored zero goals.
- **How:** start with high tempo; attack the back three directly.

### Strategy 2: Push hard between 60' and 75'
- **Evidence:** leads lost at 63', 64', 70'/73', 75'.
- **How:** bring on attacking substitutes around 60'; long balls and crosses into the box; win the second balls.

### Strategy 3: Run at Linari and the back three
- **Evidence:** Linari PAC 67; USA pace scored five.
- **How:** a quick striker running between the centre-backs; through balls behind the wing-backs.

### Strategy 4: Press the midfield in numbers
- **Evidence:** Giugliano, Caruso and Greggi have DRI 85–87 — they beat a single presser.
- **How:** two players on the ball-carrier; cut the passing lane to Giugliano.

### Strategy 5: Attack behind the wing-backs
- **Evidence:** Di Guglielmo (PAC 90) pushes high; she rarely scores (SHO 41).
- **How:** win the ball and play quickly into the space behind her.

### Strategy 6: Defend set pieces and Girelli in the box
- **Evidence:** Girelli's goals (33' and 90' at the Euro); Boattin and Giugliano deliveries.
- **How:** strong zonal marking; tight man-marking on Girelli.

## 13. Conclusion

Italy are a **difficult, compact, technical team** that came within seconds of the Euro 2025 final. Their midfield trio (Giugliano, Caruso, Greggi) is among the best in Europe on the ball.

But the 2025-26 record is stark: **no wins in eight games against top-15 opponents**, **four goals scored**, and **four leads lost** between the 63rd and 75th minutes. Italy will not beat themselves early — but they will not score much either.

> **Score first against Italy, push hard after the hour, and they have no way back.**
