# Can Arda Güler and Bernardo Silva Replace Toni Kroos at Real Madrid?

The simplest answer is:

**Not directly.**

Arda Güler and Bernardo Silva cannot individually become Toni Kroos.

But together, within the right tactical structure, they can potentially replace many of the functions Kroos once provided.

That distinction is crucial.

Because Toni Kroos was never just a passer.

He was Real Madrid’s:

**build-up controller**

**tempo controller**

**press-resistant outlet**

**long-range distributor**

**game-state manager**

Replacing him therefore cannot simply mean finding another midfielder who completes 90 percent of his passes.

The real challenge is much deeper:

> **How can Real Madrid recreate Kroos’s control without having another Kroos?**

Under Mourinho 2.0, the most realistic answer may be to distribute those responsibilities across several players — with **Bernardo Silva and Arda Güler at the center of that process.**

---

# 1. First, What Did Toni Kroos Actually Do?

To understand whether Güler and Bernardo can replace Kroos, we first need to define what Kroos actually gave Madrid.

His greatest skill was not passing accuracy.

It was **controlling the speed of the match.**

Kroos knew when to accelerate.

He knew when to recycle possession.

He knew when to draw the opponent forward.

He knew when to switch the ball 40 meters.

And perhaps most importantly, he knew when **not** to attack.

A typical Kroos sequence looked something like this:

```text
Opponent Press
      ↓

    KROOS

   ↙  ↓  ↘

CB   CM   Full-back

      ↓

Opponent shifts

      ↓

Kroos receives again

      ↓

SWITCH
```

He manipulated the opposition through positioning and circulation.

He often changed the game without dribbling past anyone.

He moved the opponent by moving the ball.

That is extremely difficult to replace.

---

# 2. Kroos Must Be Replaced by Functions, Not by Name

A useful way to understand the problem is to divide Kroos’s role into five areas:

```text
TONI KROOS

1. Build-up
2. Tempo control
3. Press resistance
4. Long distribution
5. Game-state management
```

Now we can ask a better question.

Not:

> “Can Bernardo become Kroos?”

or:

> “Can Güler become Kroos?”

But:

> **Which Kroos functions can Bernardo provide, and which can Güler provide?**

That is where the answer becomes much more interesting.

---

# 3. Bernardo Silva Is Closest to Kroos in Terms of Control

Bernardo Silva does not play like Kroos.

Their methods are very different.

Kroos was a large-space controller.

Bernardo is more of a small-space controller.

Kroos often escaped pressure like this:

```text
PRESS
  ↓
KROOS
  ↓
40m SWITCH
  ↓
Opponent block moves
```

Bernardo is more likely to do this:

```text
PRESS
  ↓
BERNARDO
  ↓
PROTECT BALL
  ↓
SHORT PASS
  ↓
MOVE
  ↓
RECEIVE AGAIN
```

Both can neutralize pressure.

But they do it differently.

Kroos changes the **space**.

Bernardo absorbs the **pressure**.

That difference matters.

---

# 4. Bernardo’s Greatest Gift May Be the Pause

Modern Real Madrid have many players whose first instinct is vertical.

Valverde sees space and attacks it.

Camavinga sees pressure and carries through it.

Bellingham sees the box and runs into it.

Mbappé sees depth and attacks behind the defence.

Vinícius sees a defender and tries to beat him.

Bernardo can provide something different:

**pause.**

```text
Space appears
      ↓
Bernardo receives
      ↓
Does not force attack
      ↓
Keeps possession
      ↓
Waits
      ↓
Madrid reorganize
      ↓
Attack later
```

This may sound less exciting.

But it is one of the most important qualities in knockout football.

A great team must know not only how to attack.

It must know **when not to attack.**

That was one of Kroos’s greatest strengths.

Bernardo can restore part of that intelligence.

---

# 5. Güler Can Replace the Creative and Progressive Side of Kroos

Arda Güler offers something different.

He is naturally more aggressive.

More imaginative.

More final-third oriented.

His instinct is to break lines.

```text
GÜLER receives

       ↓

SCAN

   ↙   ↓   ↘

Vini  Mbappé  Bellingham
```

His strengths include:

**vision**

**through balls**

**left-footed progressive passing**

**half-space creativity**

**final-third imagination**

Güler is not yet a 90-minute controller in the Kroos sense.

But he can become the player who turns stable possession into dangerous possession.

That matters just as much.

---

# 6. Güler’s Real Evolution Must Be From No.10 to No.8

This may be the most important part of the entire project.

If Güler remains only an attacking midfielder, he will never truly solve Madrid’s post-Kroos problem.

But if he evolves into a deeper creative midfielder, everything changes.

The traditional version of Güler looks like this:

```text
        Güler

behind the striker
```

The more interesting version looks like this:

```text
        Bellingham

Güler              Valverde

        Tchouaméni
```

From there, Güler can contribute to:

**build-up**

**progressive passing**

**tempo variation**

**central creativity**

**press resistance**

In other words, the next step is not simply becoming a better No.10.

It is becoming a **creative No.8**.

Not in the same way.

# 8. The One Major Kroos Quality They Still Do Not Fully Replace

There is still one area where Kroos remains difficult to replicate:

# Long-range structural control

Kroos could dominate the entire width of the pitch.

```text
LEFT SIDE

   KROOS
       \
        \
         \
          → RIGHT SIDE
```

One pass could move an entire defensive block.

Bernardo is more comfortable controlling shorter distances.

Güler is naturally more vertical and attacking.

Neither player is currently a perfect replacement for Kroos’s ability to dominate 40- or 50-meter spaces from deep.

That is where another player becomes extremely important.

---

# 9. Trent Alexander-Arnold Can Provide the Missing Long Distribution

This is where Trent becomes central to the post-Kroos architecture.

Trent can provide:

**long passing**

**diagonal switches**

**deep progression**

**build-up distribution**

That means Kroos’s old responsibilities can be split across three players.

```text
        KROOS FUNCTIONS

              ↓

    ┌─────────┼─────────┐

 Bernardo    Güler     Trent

 Control    Creation  Distribution
```

This may be the real answer.

Madrid do not need one player to replace Kroos.

They can replace Kroos **as a system**.

---

# 11. Bernardo + Güler Could Make Madrid Much Better Against High Pressing

One of the biggest benefits would be press resistance.

Imagine the opponent pressing aggressively:

```text
        Opponent Press
          ↓   ↓   ↓

Bernardo ← Güler → Trent
```

The opponent now faces multiple problems.

If they press Bernardo:

**Güler may become free.**

If they press Güler:

**Bernardo can control possession.**

If they overload both central players:

**Trent can switch the ball.**

```text
Press Bernardo?
      ↓
Güler free

Press Güler?
      ↓
Bernardo free

Press both?
      ↓
Trent switch
```

That creates a much healthier build-up structure than relying on one deep playmaker.

---

# 12. Mbappé Could Benefit Enormously

Mbappé does not need the ball constantly.

Güler can provide the direct solution:

```text

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

```

Bernardo can create the conditions:

```text

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
Space appears
  ↓
Mbappé attacks
```

Their functions complement each other.

Bernardo can help **create the space**.

Güler can help **attack the space with the pass**.

That is potentially ideal for Mbappé.

---

# 13. Vinícius Could Benefit in a Different Way

Bernardo’s presence on one side can also help Vinícius isolate defenders.

If Bernardo keeps possession on the right:

```text

     ↓

Opponent shifts →
```

The left side opens.

Then:

```text
Trent / Güler
      ↓
SWITCH
      ↓
VINÍCIUS
      ↓
1v1
```

This recreates one of the major advantages Kroos used to provide.

Kroos frequently helped move the defensive block before releasing the left side.

Now Madrid may recreate that effect collectively.

# 14. There Is One Major Defensive Problem

The Bernardo-Güler combination is technically attractive.

But neither is a defensive midfielder.

That means the structure behind them becomes critical.

A possible shape:

```text
       Güler      Bernardo

          Bellingham

         Tchouaméni
```

Or:

```text
Güler        Bernardo

    Valverde

   Tchouaméni
```

Tchouaméni becomes essential.

His responsibilities include:

**central protection**

**transition defence**

**second balls**

**covering full-backs**

**holding position**

If Bernardo and Güler control the ball, Tchouaméni must control the risk.

---

# 15. Valverde May Be Almost as Important as Tchouaméni

Valverde gives the system another layer of protection.

Bernardo and Güler provide technical control.

Valverde provides athletic control.

He can cover the spaces they cannot.

```text
Güler        Bernardo

      Valverde
     ↙   ↓   ↘

covering large spaces
```

This is why a highly functional midfield could contain several layers:

```text
          Bellingham

Güler                 Bernardo

          Valverde

        Tchouaméni
```

The exact formation may change.

The functional balance is what matters.

# Conclusion: Can Güler + Bernardo Replace Toni Kroos?

The answer is:

# Yes — but only functionally, not individually.

Bernardo Silva cannot become Toni Kroos.

Arda Güler cannot simply become Toni Kroos either.

But together, supported by the right surrounding players, they can replace a large part of what Kroos gave Real Madrid.

The structure could be:

```text
TONI KROOS

Tempo
Control
Progression
Creativity
Press Resistance
Game Management

            ↓

        DISTRIBUTED


BERNARDO SILVA

Control
Tempo
Press Resistance
Experience

            +

ARDA GÜLER

Creativity
Progression
Vision
Final Pass

            +

TRENT

Long Distribution

            +

TCHOUAMÉNI

Protection

            +

VALVERDE

Coverage
```

That may be Mourinho’s most realistic post-Kroos solution.

The Kroos-era model was:

> **One player controls the game.**

The Mourinho 2.0 model may become:

> **The system controls the game.**

Bernardo provides the calm.

Güler provides the imagination.

Trent provides the range.

Tchouaméni provides the protection.

Valverde provides the coverage.

And all of that control serves one final purpose:

```text
CONTROL
   ↓
DRAW OPPONENT FORWARD
   ↓
CREATE SPACE
   ↓
GÜLER / TRENT PASS
   ↓
VINÍCIUS / MBAPPÉ RUN
   ↓
EXPLOSION
```

Real Madrid therefore do not necessarily need to find **the next Toni Kroos**.

They need to build a system capable of reproducing what Kroos gave them.

And in that system, **Bernardo Silva + Arda Güler may become the most important technical partnership of Mourinho’s second Real Madrid era.**
