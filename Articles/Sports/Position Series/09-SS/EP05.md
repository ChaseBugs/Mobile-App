# SERIES 09 — The Second Striker

# EP05 — The Return of Two Strikers?

## Lautaro & Thuram and the Modern Front Two

22 April 2024, San Siro. The Milan derby.

Inter beat Milan 2-1 to seal the 2023-24 Serie A title.

At their rival's home ground. Against their rival.

Acerbi headed the opener, and Thuram scored the second.

That season, Inter's attack was led by two strikers:

**Lautaro Martínez and Marcus Thuram.**

Lautaro was the league's top scorer.

Two years later, in 2025-26,

Inter won Serie A again.

In the 2-0 win over Parma that sealed the title, Thuram scored and Lautaro provided an assist.

**The front two is coming back.**

But it is not the front two of the 2000s.

---

# 1. The Old Front Two vs the Modern Front Two

```text
2000s FRONT TWO                     2020s FRONT TWO
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TARGET + SECOND STRIKER             TWO MULTI-FUNCTIONAL FORWARDS
ONE HOLDS, ONE CREATES              BOTH PRESS
WAIT WHEN THE BALL IS LOST          FIRST DEFENDERS WHEN THE BALL IS LOST
4-4-2                               3-5-2 / 4-4-2 / 3-4-1-2
```

The biggest difference in the modern front two is **defending.**

Both forwards press the opposing centre-backs, block passing angles and drop into midfield to defend.

# BOTH STRIKERS DEFEND

---

# 2. Why Has the Front Two Returned? — ① The Rise of the Back Three

There are reasons for the return of the front two.

The first is **the rise of back-three systems.**

According to the Premier League's own analysis, more teams used a back three in 2019-20, and the share of starting formations with two forwards up front also rose slightly that season.

```text
3-5-2

      ST    ST
WB  CM  No.6  CM  WB
    CB   CB   CB
         GK
```

In a 3-5-2, the wing-backs cover the flanks, so the team can play two forwards up front.

And there are still three players in central midfield.

**It solves the “3 vs 2 in midfield” problem we saw in EP03.**

# THREE AT THE BACK, TWO UP FRONT

---

# 3. Why Has It Returned? — ② To Press a Back Three

The second reason is **pressing.**

When the opponent builds up with three centre-backs, it is hard to press them with a lone striker.

```text
ONE-STRIKER PRESS      TWO-STRIKER PRESS

    ST                   ST    ST
CB  CB  CB            CB   CB   CB
→ 1 vs 3              → 2 vs 3
```

With two forwards, a team can disrupt the opponent's build-up far more effectively.

**The front two is an attacking tactic — and a pressing tactic at the same time.**

---

# 4. Why Has It Returned? — ③ Numbers in the Box

The third reason is **numbers in the box.**

In a one-striker system, usually only one forward is in the box.

When a cross comes in, he has to fight two centre-backs.

With a front two, a team can create **2 vs 2** in the box.

In the 2025 Premier League season, Leeds United switched to a two-striker system, and Calvert-Lewin's big chances rose sharply — a change that turned their season around.

# MORE BODIES IN THE BOX

---

# 5. Inter — The Model of the Modern Front Two

The clearest model of the modern front two is **Inter.**

### LuLa — Lukaku & Lautaro (2020-21)

In Antonio Conte's 3-5-2, Lukaku and Lautaro led Inter to the 2020-21 Serie A title.

Lukaku scored 30 goals in all competitions and was named Serie A's Player of the Year.

Lautaro scored 19 in all competitions.

The two created 29 chances for each other, eight of which became goals — the most effective partnership in the league that season.

Conte did not use Lukaku as a simple target man.

He let him receive the ball, turn and run with it.

### 21 February 2021 — The Derby

Milan and Inter met in the middle of the title race.

Lautaro scored twice, and Lukaku once.

Inter won **3-0.**

From that game on, Inter held on to first place and won their first Scudetto in 11 years.

### ThuLa — Thuram & Lautaro (2023 onwards)

After Lukaku left, Inter signed Marcus Thuram.

```text
THURAM                       LAUTARO
──────────────────────────────────────────
RUNS INTO SPACE              FINISHES IN THE BOX
WINS PHYSICAL DUELS          DROPS DEEP TO LINK PLAY
DRIFTS WIDE                  LEADS THE PRESS
```

When Thuram drifts wide, Lautaro takes the centre.

When Lautaro drops deep, Thuram runs in behind.

**The two of them constantly swap roles.**

That is the biggest difference between the modern front two and the old one.

In the past, **the roles were fixed.**

Today, **the roles are exchanged.**

# ROLES ROTATE, NOT FIXED

### 16 September 2023 — 5-1

Thuram's first Milan derby for Inter.

In the 38th minute, he collected Dumfries' pass, cut inside his defender and curled the ball from the left of the box into the far top corner.

Inter won **5-1** — their fifth straight derby win over Milan.

**In his very first derby, Thuram proved himself to the Inter supporters.**

### The 2025 Champions League

In the 2025 Champions League semi-final, Inter met Barcelona.

The tie finished 7-6 on aggregate, with Inter winning the second leg 4-3 after extra time.

Inter were in the final.

But in Munich, they lost **5-0** to PSG.

**The modern front two was strong — but that final also showed football's next step.** PSG dominated without a fixed No.9, with every forward constantly changing position.

Inter won Serie A in 2023-24 and 2025-26,

and reached the Champions League final in 2025.

# 6. Atlético — The Team That Kept the 4-4-2

Simeone's Atlético Madrid never abandoned the front two, even in the lone-striker era.

Diego Costa, Griezmann, Morata and Julián Álvarez.

Atlético's forwards always did two jobs:

**With the ball, they scored goals.**

**Without it, they formed the first line of defence just in front of the midfield.**

```text
ATLÉTICO OUT OF POSSESSION

  ST    ST     ← THE FIRST DEFENSIVE LINE
M   M   M   M
D   D   D   D
```

In this system, Griezmann became the complete version of the **modern Second Striker** we saw in EP04.

---

# 7. What Does the Modern Second Striker Look Like?

So what does the Second Striker look like inside the modern front two?

```text
2000s SECOND STRIKER              2020s SECOND STRIKER
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WAITS BETWEEN THE LINES           MOVES BETWEEN THE LINES
BARELY DEFENDS                    LEADS THE PRESS
FIXED ROLE                        SWAPS ROLES WITH HIS PARTNER
CREATES + SCORES                  CREATES + SCORES + PRESSES + LINKS
```

Lautaro is a good example.

He finishes as a top scorer — but he also drops deep to link play and leads the press.

**The modern Second Striker is no longer the “second” forward.**

**Depending on the situation, either forward can be the first or the second.**

# THERE IS NO FIRST OR SECOND ANYMORE

---

# 8. Will the Front Two Become the Default Again?

That does not mean the front two is likely to become football's default again.

The 4-3-3 and 4-2-3-1 are still the most commonly used systems.

But the front two has once again become **a competitive option.**

It is especially effective for:

**Teams that use a back three**

**Teams that want an aggressive high press**

**Teams that want more players in the box**

**Teams with two forwards who suit each other**

The last condition is the most important.

A front two is completed not by **a system**, but by **a partnership.**

# PARTNERSHIP > FORMATION

The Second Striker disappeared, was split apart, and is now coming back.

But the player who has returned is not the same as before.

---

# Conclusion — The Return of the Second Forward

The Second Striker was a position created by a system.

When the 4-4-2 disappeared, his place shrank.

But when football began to want two forwards again, the role came back in a new form:

**A forward who runs more, presses more and constantly swaps roles with his partner.**

And the final idea of the whole series is this:

> **The Second Striker is not a position. He is a relationship.
> Whenever one forward makes another more dangerous, the Second Striker is there.**
