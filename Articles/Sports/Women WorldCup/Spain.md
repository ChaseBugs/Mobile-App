## 5. Head Coach — Sonia Bermúdez

```text
Former Spain international (Barcelona, Atlético, Levante)
Coach of Spain U19 → U20 → U23
Aug 2025  Replaced Montse Tomé after the Euro 2025 final defeat
Dec 2025  Nations League winners (beat Germany 3-0)
Jun 2026  World Cup qualification; 4-0 over England
```

### Profile
- **Counter-press first.** Her U23 sessions centred on the seconds after losing the ball — "pressing aggressively in the first few seconds can quickly win the ball back and create a dangerous attack."
- **Funnels opponents wide** and tells the nearest players to prioritise pressure over marking.
- **Self-critical and adaptive:** after the 0-0 in Germany she admitted Spain "struggled to adjust their pressing in the first half" — then won the second leg 3-0.
- **Set pieces and efficiency** were her focus before the June 2026 England game.

## 6. Tactics and Formation

### 6.1 Shapes

```text
IN POSSESSION (2-3-5)

   PINA        PARALLUELO / ESTHER        ATHENEA / VICKY LÓPEZ    ← FRONT THREE ON THE LAST LINE
        PUTELLAS                    BONMATÍ                          ← TWO "10s" BETWEEN THE LINES
  CARMONA            GUIJARRO              BATLLE                    ← FULL-BACKS VERY HIGH, GUIJARRO SCREENS
           PAREDES               ALEIXANDRI / CODINA                 ← TWO CBs SPLIT WIDE
                         CATA COLL
```

```text
AFTER LOSING THE BALL (IMMEDIATE COUNTER-PRESS)

  nearest 3–4 players swarm the ball within ~5 seconds
  → if it fails: drop into a 4-4-2 / 4-1-4-1 mid-block
```

```text
MID-BLOCK (4-4-2)

          PARALLUELO      PUTELLAS
   PINA        BONMATÍ   GUIJARRO      ATHENEA
  CARMONA   PAREDES   ALEIXANDRI   BATLLE
                CATA COLL
```

### 6.2 Possession — How They Break You Down

| Mechanism | Detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Overload the centre | Putellas and Bonmatí receive between the lines; Guijarro (DEF 84, PHY 88) recycles | vs England (Jun 2026): Walsh said Spain had "bodies everywhere" |
| Pin the full-backs | Wingers stay wide and high; Batlle/Carmona overlap | Batlle's cross → Mariona header (Euro final 25') |
| Long shots from midfield | Guijarro scores from distance | 25-yard deflected goal vs England (19'); long-range opener vs USA (3') |
| Late runs into the box | Putellas arrives at the far post | Tap-in vs USA (27', Oct 2026) |

### 6.3 Pressing — Where They Win the Ball

- **Goalkeeper errors are punished:** Paralluelo scored after US goalkeeper Tullis-Joyce misplayed the ball (Oct 2026).
- **Counter-press stops counters before they start** — England had zero shots on goal in Mallorca.

### 6.4 Scoring Bursts

- **Three goals in 13 minutes** vs Germany (61'–74', Dec 2025).
- **Two goals in 11 minutes** vs the USA (3', 14') and vs England (37', 55' around half-time).

### 6.5 Set Pieces

- **For:** Paredes is the main target; Guijarro hit the bar from a corner (Jun 2026).
- **Against:** the 0-1 at Wembley came from **a corner in the 3rd minute** (Russo flick → Hemp); Imade's late header chance came from a Spain corner, but defensively they were exposed by England's set play.

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Eight Games Against Strong Opponents

### Game ① 31 July 2023, World Cup Group — Japan 4-0 Spain (Wellington) — the blueprint for beating Spain

| Minute | Goal |
|---|---|
| 10'/12' | Miyazawa (Kumagai assist) |
| 29' | Ueki (deflected) |
| 40' | Miyazawa |
| 82' | Tanaka |

**What Japan did:** gave Spain the ball, defended in a compact 3-4-3/5-4-1, and attacked the space behind Spain's high full-backs with Miyazawa's diagonal runs.
**Lesson:** **Possession is not control.** Spain's high full-backs and non-defending wingers leave the flanks open.

### Game ② 6 August 2024, Olympic SF — Brazil 4-2 Spain (Marseille)

- **5'** Paredes own goal after **Cata Coll's error under Brazil's press**; Portilho 45+3'; Adriana header; Kerolin 90+1'.
**Lesson:** **Press Spain's goalkeeper and centre-backs on goal kicks** — they will try to play out, and errors come.

### Game ③ 23 July 2025, Euro SF — Spain 1-0 Germany (aet, Zurich)

- 0-0 for 112 minutes; Bonmatí scored from a tight angle at the near post (113'); Cata Coll made a late double save.
**Lesson:** Germany's deep, physical block **held Spain for almost two hours.** Spain need a moment of genius to break a well-drilled defence.

### Game ④ 27 July 2025, Euro Final — England 1-1 Spain (England won 3-1 on pens, Basel)

| Minute | Moment |
|---|---|
| 25' | **Mariona** header from **Ona Batlle's cross** — England's defence flat-footed |
| 57' | **Russo** header from Kelly's cross |
| Pens | **Hampton saved Mariona and Bonmatí**; Paralluelo dragged wide; Kelly scored the winner |

**Lessons:**
- Spain's **full-back crosses** are a real weapon.
- In a shootout, **Spain's stars missed three of four.**

### Game ⑤ 28 Nov & 2 Dec 2025, Nations League Final — Germany 0-0 Spain, Spain 3-0 Germany

- First leg: Spain could not adjust their press in the first half (Bermúdez).
- Second leg: **Pina 61', López 68', Pina 74'** — three in 13 minutes once Germany tired.
**Lesson:** Spain **adapt between games**, and they score in bursts in the last 30 minutes as opponents tire from chasing.

### Game ⑥ 14 April 2026, WCQ — England 1-0 Spain (Wembley)

| Minute | Moment |
|---|---|
| 3' | **Hemp** — corner → Russo flick → Hemp; Putellas couldn't clear on the line (goal by one inch) |
| 19' | Hemp hit the post (Bronze backheel) |
| 2nd half | Carmona and López hit the woodwork; Hampton saved from Guijarro and Imade |

Keira Walsh: **"Our plan was to sit in a low block and when we had a chance, go forward."**
**Lesson:** **Early set-piece goal + disciplined low block + an excellent goalkeeper** = the recipe that works.

### Game ⑦ 5 June 2026, WCQ — Spain 4-0 England (Mallorca) — what happens if you try to play

- Guijarro 19' (25 yards), Putellas 37' and 55', Pina 78'. **England had no shot on goal.**
- Wiegman: England should have "moved players into the pockets between Spain's lines"; Walsh: Spain had "bodies everywhere," making it impossible to play out.
**Lesson:** **Do not try to play out against Spain's counter-press.** England tried — and suffered their heaviest defeat under Wiegman.

### Game ⑧ 10 & 13 October 2026 — USA 0-2 Spain, USA 2-2 Spain

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

### Strategy ① — Low Block, Deny the Turn
- **Evidence:** England (Apr 2026), Japan (2023), Germany (112 minutes at Euro 2025).
- **Instructions:**
  - Two compact banks of four/five, 30–35 metres deep.
  - Midfielders stay **touch-tight behind Putellas and Bonmatí** — make them receive facing their own goal.
  - Close Guijarro at 25 metres — she shoots from distance.

### Strategy ② — Do Not Play Out Short
- **Evidence:** England's 0-4 in Mallorca when they tried to build from the back.
- **Instructions:** goal kicks long to a target striker; midfield wins second balls.

### Strategy ③ — Set Pieces
- **Evidence:** Wembley 3' corner goal; Euro final header.
- **Instructions:** rehearse near-post flick-ons and far-post runs; every corner is a big chance.

### Strategy ④ — Counter Behind Batlle and Carmona

```text
SPAIN FULL-BACKS HIGH  +  WINGERS DON'T TRACK BACK (DEF 25–47)
                     ↓
WIN THE BALL → ONE PASS WIDE → RUN AT PAREDES (PAC 74)
```

### Strategy ⑤ — Press the Goal Kick at Chosen Moments
- **Evidence:** Cata Coll's error vs Brazil; Spain punish keeper errors too, so press only when they are set up short.

### Strategy ⑥ — Stay in the Game, Then Take Penalties
- **Evidence:** Spain's stars missed three of four in the Euro final shootout.

### Strategy ⑦ — Push in the Second Half
- **Evidence:** USA came back from 0-2 (Oct 2026) with direct, athletic attacks once Spain eased off.

### Summary

```text
LOW BLOCK + DENY THE TURN (PUTELLAS, BONMATÍ, GUIJARRO AT 25m)
                       +
NO SHORT BUILD-UP — GO LONG
                       +
SET PIECES (FLICK-ONS)
                       +
COUNTERS BEHIND THE HIGH FULL-BACKS
                       +
SURVIVE → PENALTIES
                       ↓
MAKE SPAIN DOMINATE — AND STILL LOSE
```

## 13. Conclusion

Spain are the world's best team. But they have a **repeatable weakness profile**:

1. **Set pieces against them**
2. **Deep blocks + counters into the space behind their full-backs**
3. **Pressure on their goalkeeper**
4. **Shootouts**

> **You will not out-pass Spain. Out-wait them, hit them from a set piece or a counter, and take them to penalties.**
