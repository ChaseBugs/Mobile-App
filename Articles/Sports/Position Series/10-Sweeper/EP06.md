# SERIES 10 — The Sweeper

# EP06 — SPECIAL: The Libero Reborn

## Stones, Bastoni, Neuer — and the Conclusion of the Position Series

Chelsea, 2016-17.

After a few early-season defeats, Antonio Conte switched his system to a **3-4-3.**

In the centre of the back three stood **David Luiz**, just back from PSG.

Many people had doubts.

David Luiz had a reputation as an unreliable defender.

But in the centre of a back three, he became a completely different player.

Between Azpilicueta and Cahill, he covered the space behind,

and started attacks with accurate long passes.

Chelsea kept the most clean sheets in the league and won the Premier League.

David Luiz was named in the PFA Team of the Year.

**A player between two defenders who covers and distributes.**

It looked very much like a name we had known for a long time:

# LIBERO

---

# 1. The Five Functions of the Libero

As we saw in EP01, the Libero's job can be broken down into five functions.

```text
THE LIBERO'S FUNCTIONS

1. PROTECT THE SPACE BEHIND THE DEFENSIVE LINE   (SWEEPING)
2. COVER WHEN A TEAMMATE IS BEATEN               (COVERING)
3. STEP FORWARD WITH THE BALL                    (CARRYING)
4. START THE BUILD-UP                            (DISTRIBUTING)
5. DIRECT THE DEFENSIVE LINE                     (ORGANISING)
```

In modern football, these five functions have been divided among different players.

In this SPECIAL, we look at **the modern heirs of the Libero.**

---

# 2. Manuel Neuer — The Libero Behind the Line

## SWEEPING

As we saw in SERIES 08, EP03, Neuer performed the Libero's job of protecting **the space behind the defensive line** — from the goalkeeper's position.

Against Algeria at the 2014 World Cup, he kept racing out of his box to stop counter-attacks.

**“The goalkeeper is playing like a libero.”**

That was exactly what he was doing.

```text
LIBERO (1970s)            NEUER (2010s)
COVERS BEHIND THE DF  →   COVERS FROM THE GK POSITION
```

**The Libero's “sweeping” function passed to the goalkeeper.**

---

# 3. Leonardo Bonucci — The Centre of a Back Three

## COVERING + DISTRIBUTING

From 2011, Conte's Juventus played a 3-5-2.

Barzagli, Bonucci and Chiellini — a back three known as **“BBC.”**

In the middle, Bonucci covered for the two defenders beside him and started attacks with accurate long passes.

As we saw in SERIES 07, EP03, he was called **“the quarterback centre-back.”**

In 2013-14, Juventus won Serie A with a record **102 points.**

```text
BARZAGLI       BONUCCI           CHIELLINI
(STOPPER)  (COVER + DISTRIBUTE)  (STOPPER)
```

**Two stoppers with one free man between them — this is the modern version of the Libero system.**

David Luiz played the same role for Chelsea in 2016-17.

# THE LIBERO IN A BACK THREE

---

# 4. Busquets and Rodri — The No.6 Who Drops

## DISTRIBUTING

As we saw in SERIES 03, the modern No.6 drops **between the two centre-backs** during the build-up.

```text
NORMAL POSITION           IN BUILD-UP

     No.6
 CB        CB             CB   No.6   CB   ← EFFECTIVELY A BACK THREE
```

Busquets played this role at Guardiola's Barcelona,

and Rodri at Manchester City.

Rodri won the Ballon d'Or in 2024.

**The Libero's job of starting the build-up from within the defensive line passed to the midfielder.**

---

# 5. John Stones — From the Defensive Line Into Midfield

## CARRYING

Istanbul, 2023.

Without the ball, Stones was a centre-back. With it, he was a midfielder next to Rodri.

```text
OUT OF POSSESSION: CB
IN POSSESSION:     CB → MIDFIELD
```

Beckenbauer started **behind** the defensive line and stepped forward.

Stones started **inside** the defensive line and stepped forward.

City won the treble that season.

**The Libero's “carrying” function passed to the hybrid centre-back.**

# THE HYBRID DEFENDER

---

# 6. Alessandro Bastoni — The Centre-Back Who Attacks

## CARRYING + ATTACKING

In Inter's 3-5-2, Bastoni is the left centre-back.

But in the build-up, he steps into midfield,

and sometimes runs down the flank to the opponent's box to cross or link play.

He is the **first creative outlet** of Inter's build-up, averaging around 11 progressive passes per 90 minutes.

Inter won Serie A in 2025-26.

```text
INTER'S BUILD-UP

    BASTONI ───→ MIDFIELD / FLANK / BOX
       ↑
(LEFT CENTRE-BACK)
```

Bastoni is one of the modern defenders closest to Beckenbauer's “advancing Libero.”

---

# 7. Virgil van Dijk — The Conductor of the Line

## ORGANISING

The Libero's last function is **command.**

As we saw in SERIES 07, EP05, Van Dijk organised Liverpool's entire defensive line.

He set the height of the line, directed his teammates' positions, and steered the opposing forwards where he wanted them to go.

Just as Baresi once directed Sacchi's line.

# 9. So Has the Libero Really Disappeared?

This series' answer is:

**The Libero as a position has disappeared.**

A fixed free man behind the defensive line is almost never seen in modern football.

**But the Libero as a function is more widespread than ever.**

The goalkeeper sweeps.

The centre of the back three covers.

The No.6 drops in to build.

The centre-back steps into midfield.

The defensive leader directs the line.

> **The Libero has not disappeared. In modern football, every defender has been given a little of the Libero's freedom.**

# FROM POSITIONS TO FUNCTIONS

# Conclusion — From One Free Man to Eleven

In the 1930s, Rappan gave one defender his freedom.

In the 1970s, Beckenbauer used that freedom to rule the game.

In the late 1980s, Sacchi took that freedom away.

And modern football has shared that freedom out **among every player.**

Centre-backs become midfielders.

Goalkeepers become defenders.

Full-backs become playmakers.

Forwards become the first defenders.

**In modern football, every player is a little bit of a Libero.**

> **The history of football began with one free man.
> Now, football wants eleven of them.**
