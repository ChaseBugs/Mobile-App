# Netherlands — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## Oranje Leeuwinnen

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> ⚠️ **Qualification status:** not yet qualified. The Netherlands finished **second in League A Group 2** (behind France, 11 points) and entered the UEFA play-offs. They beat **Hungary 8-0 and 3-0** in round 1 (October 2026). Round 2 is against **Slovenia (26 November / 5 December 2026)**; the winner qualifies.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how the Netherlands play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

**One-line verdict:** A technically excellent, **unbeaten-in-big-games** side under its new coach: they **beat France 2-1**, drew 1-1 in France, and beat Canada 1-0. They have the **record striker Miedema** (87), two Barcelona/Real Madrid forwards with **PAC 87–90**, and a deep midfield. But they **lose concentration late**: points dropped to Poland (84') and Ireland (90') left them second behind France. Their centre-backs are **not quick** and the full-backs push very high.

## 5. Head Coach — Arjan Veurink

- Took over after Euro 2025. Long-time assistant to Sarina Wiegman (Netherlands, Euro 2017 title and 2019 World Cup final; then England).
- **Record:** W 8 D 3 L 1.
- **Approach:** possession-based 4-3-3, with full use of the squad's depth — "the result underlines the team's depth" (after beating France without seven injured players).
- He credited the France win to "how the group translated the game plan onto the pitch and the level of passion they played with."

## 6. Tactics and Formation

### 6.1 Shapes

```text
IN POSSESSION (4-3-3 → 2-3-5)

   PELOVA / KAPTEIN        MIEDEMA / LEUCHTER        BEERENSTEYN
               ROORD                      VAN DE DONK / KAPTEIN
                         EGURROLA
   CASPARIJ / WILMS (high)  JANSSEN    BUURMAN / DIJKSTRA    BRUGTS (high)
                         VAN DOMSELAAR

OUT OF POSSESSION (4-4-2 MID BLOCK / 4-1-4-1)

            MIEDEMA            ROORD
   BEERENSTEYN    EGURROLA     KAPTEIN     PELOVA
   CASPARIJ    JANSSEN      BUURMAN       BRUGTS
                         VAN DOMSELAAR
```

### 6.2 Build-Up
- **Egurrola** (OL Lyonnes, PAS 82) — the pivot; **Roord** (PAS 85) and **van de Donk** (DRI 85) between the lines.
- **Van Domselaar** (Arsenal, KIC 83) is comfortable building from the back.
- **Janssen** (left-footed, PAS 74) switches play to the right-back.

### 6.3 Attacking Entry
1. **Beerensteyn's pace in behind** — PAC 90 (Real Madrid). Six goals in 2025-26.
2. **Brugts** (Barcelona, PAC 87) — plays as winger or left-back; the counter-attack winner vs France.
3. **Miedema** — the all-time leading scorer; four goals in 29 minutes vs Korea.
4. **Roord's late runs** (SHO 83).
5. **Headers from midfield runners** — Kaptein's equaliser in France (76').

### 6.4 Transitions
- **Very dangerous on the counter** — Brugts's winner vs France came from a counter-attack.
- Beerensteyn and Brugts both have PAC 87+.

### 6.5 Set Pieces
- Janssen (penalties, left-foot delivery), Roord.
- Tall targets: Buurman (Chelsea), Janssen, Spitse.

### 6.6 Game-State Behaviour

| State | What the Netherlands do | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| vs top teams | Organised mid block, counter-attack | France 2-1 (counter), Canada 1-0 |
| Leading late | **Drop deeper and concede** | Poland 84', Ireland 90' |
| Trailing | Strong reaction — technical quality | Ireland: 0-1 → 1-1 (70'), 1-2 → 2-2 (80') |
| End of first half | Concede occasionally | Katoto 45+1' |

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

### Strategy 1: Stay in the game until the end — attack after 80'
- **Evidence:** Poland 84'; Ireland 89'/90'; Kamczyk pen 83'.
- **How:** keep fresh attacking substitutes for the last 15 minutes; long balls and crosses late.

### Strategy 2: Play direct and physical
- **Evidence:** Ireland 3-2; England 4-0 at the Euro.
- **How:** win first and second balls; press Roord and van de Donk physically (PAC 72–75).

### Strategy 3: Respond immediately after conceding
- **Evidence:** Larkin scored one minute after the Dutch equaliser.
- **How:** restart quickly and attack while the Dutch celebrate and reorganise.

### Strategy 4: Don't give them counter-attacks
- **Evidence:** Brugts's winner vs France; Beerensteyn PAC 90.
- **How:** keep a rest defence of three plus a holding midfielder; never push both full-backs high together.

### Strategy 5: Go around Egurrola
- **Evidence:** PAC 62.
- **How:** quick one-twos in the space beside her; your No. 10 drifts into that zone.

### Strategy 6: Asian teams — defend deep and compact
- **Evidence:** Korea Republic 0-5 (Miedema four in 29 minutes).
- **How:** no high line against Miedema and Beerensteyn; a compact two-line block.

```text
NO COUNTER-ATTACKS (rest defence ×3 + No. 6)
          +
DIRECT, PHYSICAL PLAY
          +
QUICK COMBINATIONS AROUND EGURROLA
          +
FRESH ATTACKERS FOR THE LAST 15 MINUTES
          ↓
IRELAND 3-2 — THE NETHERLANDS' ONLY DEFEAT OF 2025-26
```

## 13. Conclusion

The Netherlands have **lost one game in 12** under Arjan Veurink and beat France with seven players missing. With **Miedema, Beerensteyn, Brugts, Roord and Egurrola**, they have one of the most technical squads in the tournament and a lethal counter-attack.

But they **dropped three points in the qualifiers through late goals** and conceded immediately after scoring in Cork. Direct, physical teams that keep going until the end trouble them most.

> **Deny the Dutch counter, play direct, and keep attacking — the Netherlands crack in the final ten minutes.**
