# Why Luka Modrić Was So Important to Real Madrid

## The 2014/15 Juventus Semi-Final That Revealed His True Value

When people remember Real Madrid in the 2014/15 season, attention naturally goes to the attacking stars.

**Cristiano Ronaldo.
Karim Benzema.
Gareth Bale.**

BBC.

Behind them were technically outstanding midfielders such as **Toni Kroos, James Rodríguez and Isco**.

On paper, Real Madrid had enormous quality.

Yet the 2015 Champions League semi-final against Juventus exposed something important:

# HAVING GREAT ATTACKERS IS NOT ENOUGH.

The team still needs someone capable of connecting its different phases.

Real Madrid had one of the best players in the world at doing exactly that.

# LUKA MODRIĆ.

And against Juventus, he was missing.

Modrić did not play in either leg because of a knee injury.

Real Madrid eventually lost the tie **3–2 on aggregate**.

It would be wrong to say that Modrić's absence alone caused the elimination.

But the two matches provide an excellent case study of something deeper:

# WHAT EXACTLY DID LUKA MODRIĆ GIVE REAL MADRID?

---

# 1. Real Madrid's Midfield Had Already Changed

To understand Modrić's importance, we first need to understand what happened before the 2014/15 season.

Real Madrid's 2013/14 Champions League-winning midfield had included players such as:

**Xabi Alonso**

**Luka Modrić**

**Ángel Di María**

But during the summer of 2014, the structure changed significantly.

Xabi Alonso left for Bayern Munich.

Di María left for Manchester United.

Toni Kroos arrived from Bayern.

James Rodríguez arrived from Monaco.

Real Madrid therefore entered 2014/15 with a different midfield identity.

One important structure became:

```text
            KROOS

      MODRIĆ      JAMES

              ↓

             BBC
```

It was technically outstanding.

But it depended heavily on complementary functions.

---

# 2. Kroos and Modrić Were Not the Same Type of Midfielder

Both Kroos and Modrić are often described as playmakers.

That is true.

But it can also hide their differences.

### Toni Kroos

Kroos offered:

* Tempo Control
* Distribution
* Build-Up Organisation
* Long Passing
* Switching Play
* Circulation

### Luka Modrić

Modrić offered:

* Press Resistance
* Turning Under Pressure
* Progressive Carrying
* Dynamic Movement
* Line Breaking
* Transition Connection
* Combination Play

A useful simplification is:

```text
KROOS
CONTROL
   ↓
DISTRIBUTE


MODRIĆ
RECEIVE PRESSURE
   ↓
ESCAPE
   ↓
PROGRESS
```

Kroos could organise the game.

Modrić could move the game.

# KROOS CONTROLLED THE STRUCTURE.

# MODRIĆ MOVED THROUGH THE STRUCTURE.

Together, those qualities were extremely complementary.

---

# 3. Modrić Was the Bridge

Real Madrid possessed devastating forwards.

But forwards cannot attack until the ball reaches them.

The basic chain is simple:

```text
DEFENCE
   ↓
MIDFIELD
   ↓
ATTACK
   ↓
GOAL
```

The difficult part is often:

# MIDFIELD → ATTACK.

This was where Modrić became so valuable.

```text
BALL RECOVERY
      ↓
MODRIĆ
      ↓
TURN
      ↓
ESCAPE PRESS
      ↓
PROGRESS
      ↓
RONALDO / BENZEMA / BALE
```

Real Madrid had tremendous speed ahead of the ball.

Modrić helped give that speed a platform.

---

# 4. Press Resistance Was One of His Greatest Weapons

Imagine Modrić receiving with an opponent immediately pressing him.

```text
      OPPONENT
         ↓
      MODRIĆ
```

There are several possible solutions.

Pass backwards.

Pass sideways.

Protect possession.

All can be correct.

But Modrić possessed another solution.

# TURN.

```text
PRESS
  ↓
MODRIĆ
   ↘
    TURN
      ↓
    ESCAPE
      ↓
    SPACE
```

This looks like an individual technical action.

Tactically, however, something much more important has happened.

The opponent committed a player to the press.

Modrić eliminated that player.

Now there is space behind him.

```text
PRESSER
   X

MODRIĆ →→→

        SPACE

RONALDO   BENZEMA   BALE
```

Suddenly Real Madrid can attack.

That is why Modrić's Press Resistance was not simply beautiful technique.

It was:

# A PROGRESSION MECHANISM.

---

# 5. One Turn Could Change the Numerical Situation

Suppose an opposition midfielder presses Modrić.

Initially:

```text
11 vs 11
```

Modrić receives and turns away.

The pressing midfielder is now behind the play.

For several seconds, the attacking situation can effectively behave like:

```text
11 vs 10
```

The player has not literally disappeared.

But he has been temporarily removed from the defensive action.

This is one of the most valuable abilities an elite midfielder can possess.

A pass can break a line.

But so can:

# A TURN.

And so can:

# A CARRY.

Modrić could do both.

---

# 6. Modrić's Dribbling Had a Tactical Purpose

Modrić was not carrying the ball simply to produce beautiful moments.

His actions usually had direction.

```text
SCAN
 ↓
RECEIVE
 ↓
TURN
 ↓
ACCELERATE
 ↓
RELEASE
```

The purpose was to eliminate pressure.

Once the opponent had been beaten, Modrić normally moved the ball quickly.

That made him more than a Ball Carrier.

He could become a:

# TEMPO ACCELERATOR.

Real Madrid could move from:

```text
CONTROLLED POSSESSION
```

to:

```text
DIRECT ATTACK
```

within seconds.

---

# 7. Why This Was Perfect for Ronaldo and Bale

Cristiano Ronaldo and Gareth Bale were extraordinarily dangerous when the opponent's defensive structure was not fully organised.

Consider two situations.

### Slow progression

```text
BALL RECOVERY
      ↓
SIDE PASS
      ↓
SIDE PASS
      ↓
BACK PASS
      ↓
OPPONENT RECOVERS
      ↓
SPACE DISAPPEARS
```

Now compare it with:

```text
BALL RECOVERY
      ↓
MODRIĆ RECEIVES
      ↓
TURN
      ↓
FORWARD RELEASE
      ↓
RONALDO / BALE
      ↓
OPEN SPACE
```

The difference may only be a few seconds.

But at elite level, those seconds are enormous.

Ronaldo's run becomes more dangerous because the centre-back has not recovered.

Bale's acceleration becomes more dangerous because the full-back is still moving backwards.

Benzema can receive between disorganised lines.

Therefore:

# FAST FORWARDS NEED THE BALL BEFORE THE DEFENCE BECOMES SET.

Modrić helped make that happen.

---

# 8. The Forgotten Beginning of a Counterattack

When people remember a Real Madrid counterattack, they often remember the final image.

Ronaldo sprinting.

Bale accelerating.

Benzema combining.

But the attack had to start somewhere.

```text
BALL RECOVERY
      ↓
FIRST CLEAN ACTION
      ↓
PRESS ESCAPE
      ↓
FORWARD RELEASE
      ↓
SPRINT
      ↓
FINISH
```

Modrić was particularly valuable around:

# FIRST CLEAN ACTION

and

# PRESS ESCAPE.

Those actions rarely appear in highlight reels.

But without them, the spectacular part of the counterattack may never happen.

---

# 9. 2014 Champions League Final — Modrić Was There

One year before the Juventus elimination, Real Madrid played Atlético Madrid in the 2014 Champions League Final.

Modrić started.

Real Madrid were losing 1–0 deep into stoppage time.

Then Modrić delivered the corner.

Sergio Ramos attacked it.

# 92:48.

Goal.

1–1.

Real Madrid eventually won 4–1 after extra time.

Bale scored.

Marcelo scored.

Cristiano scored.

And Real Madrid finally won:

# LA DÉCIMA.

The corner itself should not be used as the main proof of Modrić's importance.

His influence was much broader.

But the final demonstrates something important about the 2013/14 and 2014/15 Madrid teams:

Modrić was already operating at the centre of their biggest matches.

---

# 10. Then Came the Injury

Modrić's 2014/15 season was disrupted by injuries.

He had already missed a significant part of the campaign before returning.

Then in April 2015, against Málaga, he suffered another knee injury.

This time the timing was devastating.

Real Madrid were entering the decisive phase of the Champions League.

Their semi-final opponent was Juventus.

Modrić would miss:

# THE FIRST LEG.

And:

# THE SECOND LEG.

Now Ancelotti faced a difficult problem.

Real Madrid had other excellent midfielders.

But:

# WHO COULD REPLACE MODRIĆ'S FUNCTION?

---

# 11. Juventus vs Real Madrid — First Leg

The first leg took place in Turin on 5 May 2015.

Real Madrid were missing not only Modrić but also Benzema.

Ancelotti made one of the most discussed tactical decisions of the tie:

# SERGIO RAMOS IN MIDFIELD.

Real Madrid's shape was approximately:

```text
        RONALDO      BALE

ISCO       KROOS     RAMOS      JAMES

MARCELO   VARANE     PEPE     CARVAJAL
```

Ramos was obviously an extraordinary footballer.

But he was an extraordinary:

# CENTRE-BACK.

Moving him into midfield changed the characteristics of Real Madrid's central area.

---

# 12. What Ramos Could Give the Midfield

Ramos could provide:

**Physical Strength**

**Aggression**

**Duel Winning**

**Ball Recovery**

**Aerial Ability**

**Defensive Intensity**

Those qualities could be useful against Juventus.

But the problem was not whether Ramos was a good footballer.

The problem was:

# ROLE COMPATIBILITY.

Modrić normally provided something different.

```text
RAMOS

DUEL
 ↓
RECOVER
 ↓
SECURE
```

Modrić could provide:

```text
MODRIĆ

RECEIVE
 ↓
TURN
 ↓
ESCAPE
 ↓
CARRY
 ↓
CONNECT
 ↓
PROGRESS
```

Those are completely different midfield functions.

---

# 13. You Can Replace the Player Without Replacing the Function

This is one of the most important tactical lessons from the tie.

When a player is injured, coaches obviously put another player into his position.

But:

# SAME POSITION ≠ SAME FUNCTION.

Ramos could occupy a midfield position.

That did not make him Modrić.

Likewise, James or Isco could play centrally.

But neither reproduced exactly the same combination of:

**Mobility**

**Press Resistance**

**Ball Carrying**

**Defensive Movement**

**Transition Connection**

that Modrić provided.

This is why some players are extremely difficult to replace.

Not because there are no other good players.

But because:

# THEIR COMBINATION OF FUNCTIONS IS RARE.

---

# 14. Juventus Created Problems Between the Lines

Juventus had several technically and tactically intelligent midfielders and forwards.

One particularly important player was Carlos Tévez.

Tévez could move away from the centre-backs and enter the space between Real Madrid's midfield and defence.

```text
REAL DEFENCE
────────────

    TÉVEZ
      ↓

     SPACE

────────────
REAL MIDFIELD
```

This created decisions.

Should Ramos follow him?

Should one of the centre-backs step forward?

Should Kroos cover?

Every movement changed another part of the structure.

Modrić's defensive importance was useful precisely in these kinds of situations because he was constantly adjusting his position.

His value was not purely offensive.

# MOBILITY ALSO PROTECTED THE STRUCTURE.

---

# 15. Possession Is Not Progression

Real Madrid could still possess the ball.

But there is an important difference between:

# HAVING THE BALL

and

# MOVING THE OPPONENT.

A team can circulate:

```text
CB → KROOS → WIDE
             ↓
            BACK
             ↓
           KROOS
```

without seriously disturbing the defensive block.

This is possession.

But progression requires something else.

```text
MODRIĆ RECEIVES
       ↓
OPPONENT PRESSES
       ↓
MODRIĆ TURNS
       ↓
OPPONENT POSITION BREAKS
       ↓
SPACE OPENS
```

Now the structure has changed.

This leads to a crucial distinction:

# PASS COMPLETION ≠ PROGRESSION.

And:

# POSSESSION ≠ CONTROL.

Sometimes the midfielder who destabilises the opponent is more important than the midfielder who simply keeps the ball.

---

# 16. Juventus 2–1 Real Madrid

Juventus won the first leg:

# 2–1.

Álvaro Morata opened the scoring against his former club.

Cristiano Ronaldo equalised.

Carlos Tévez later converted a penalty.

The tie remained open.

But the match demonstrated that Real Madrid's midfield did not have its normal balance.

There was still quality.

There was still attacking threat.

But there were fewer natural solutions for moving through pressure.

---

# 17. Second Leg — Benzema Returned, Modrić Did Not

The second leg took place at the Santiago Bernabéu.

This time Benzema returned.

Modrić remained unavailable.

Real Madrid used a much more attacking midfield structure.

Approximately:

```text
RONALDO      BENZEMA       BALE

      ISCO          JAMES

             KROOS
```

Look at the technical quality.

Ronaldo.

Benzema.

Bale.

James.

Isco.

Kroos.

Almost every player could create something.

But there was another problem.

# BALANCE.

Kroos now carried enormous responsibility.

---

# 18. Kroos Had Too Many Problems to Solve

Normally Kroos could concentrate on:

**Build-Up**

**Distribution**

**Tempo**

**Circulation**

But without Modrić, the team also needed solutions for:

**Press Resistance**

**Central Progression**

**Transition Connection**

**Dynamic Coverage**

**Progressive Carrying**

Kroos could perform some of these actions.

But asking one player to solve all of them changes the structure.

```text
WITH MODRIĆ

KROOS
CONTROL / DISTRIBUTE

        +

MODRIĆ
ESCAPE / CARRY / PROGRESS
```

Without Modrić:

```text
KROOS
CONTROL
   +
BUILD
   +
CONNECT
   +
PROGRESS
   +
PROTECT
```

The workload becomes concentrated.

This is one reason Modrić and Kroos complemented each other so well.

---

# 19. Modrić Made Kroos More Effective

Great partnerships do not always involve two players doing the same thing.

Often the opposite is true.

The best partnerships involve players solving different problems.

Kroos could be viewed as a:

# SPACE DISTRIBUTOR.

```text
SCAN FIELD
    ↓
IDENTIFY SPACE
    ↓
PASS INTO SPACE
```

Modrić could be viewed as a:

# PRESSURE MANIPULATOR.

```text
RECEIVE
   ↓
ATTRACT PRESSURE
   ↓
TURN
   ↓
ESCAPE
   ↓
CREATE NEW SPACE
```

Kroos saw the space.

Modrić could sometimes create it.

That distinction made their partnership extremely powerful.

---

# 20. Real Madrid 1–1 Juventus

Real Madrid took the lead in the second leg through Cristiano Ronaldo's penalty.

At that point, Madrid were temporarily heading through.

But Álvaro Morata scored again.

# 1–1.

Real Madrid pushed for another goal.

It never came.

Final aggregate:

# JUVENTUS 3–2 REAL MADRID.

Real Madrid's defence of the Champions League was over.

---

# 21. Would Modrić Have Changed the Result?

This question needs a careful answer.

# POSSIBLY.

But:

# WE CANNOT KNOW.

Football is not a mathematical simulation where adding one player automatically changes the result.

Juventus were an outstanding side.

Their squad included players such as:

**Gianluigi Buffon**

**Giorgio Chiellini**

**Leonardo Bonucci**

**Andrea Pirlo**

**Claudio Marchisio**

**Arturo Vidal**

**Paul Pogba**

**Carlos Tévez**

**Álvaro Morata**

And Massimiliano Allegri prepared the tie extremely well.

Real Madrid also had other problems during the season.

Therefore:

# MODRIĆ ABSENT = REAL MADRID LOSE

would be an oversimplification.

The better conclusion is:

# MODRIĆ ABSENT = REAL MADRID LOST IMPORTANT MIDFIELD OPTIONS.

That is tactically much more meaningful.

---

# 22. Modrić's Importance Cannot Be Measured Only Through Goals and Assists

Imagine this sequence:

```text
MODRIĆ
   ↓
BREAK PRESS

KROOS
   ↓
PROGRESSIVE PASS

BENZEMA
   ↓
FINAL PASS

RONALDO
   ↓
GOAL
```

The statistics may show:

**Goal — Ronaldo**

**Assist — Benzema**

Nothing for Modrić.

But without Modrić beating the initial pressure, the attack might never have developed.

This is why midfielders like Modrić require a different evaluation.

Instead of asking only:

# HOW MANY GOALS?

or:

# HOW MANY ASSISTS?

ask:

# WHAT CHANGED AFTER HE TOUCHED THE BALL?

Did the opponent lose its shape?

Was a pressing player eliminated?

Did Real Madrid enter the next line?

Did the forwards receive before the defence recovered?

That is where Modrić's influence becomes visible.

---

# 23. Modrić Also Connected the Team Without Touching the Ball

His intelligence was not limited to possession.

Before receiving, Modrić constantly adjusted his position.

He could move toward Carvajal.

Support Bale.

Return centrally.

Offer Kroos another passing angle.

Move into the half-space.

Then rotate again.

```text
          KROOS
            ↕
CARVAJAL ↔ MODRIĆ ↔ CENTRAL SPACE
            ↓
           BALE
```

This created:

# PASSING OPTIONS.

And passing options are the foundation of possession football.

The best midfielders do not begin thinking when they receive the ball.

# THEY PREPARE THE NEXT ACTION BEFORE THE BALL ARRIVES.

Modrić excelled at this.

---

# 24. Modrić Created Something Even More Important Than Space

Football tactics constantly discuss space.

But another resource is just as important:

# TIME.

Suppose Modrić receives under pressure.

Instead of immediately returning the ball backwards, he protects it.

Turns.

Escapes.

That may take only one second.

But during that second:

```text
MODRIĆ HOLDS PRESSURE

RONALDO → STARTS RUN

BALE → ATTACKS CHANNEL

BENZEMA → CHANGES POSITION

CARVAJAL → MOVES FORWARD
```

One second for Modrić becomes movement for four teammates.

This is a deeper way to understand Press Resistance.

# MODRIĆ DID NOT ONLY FIND SPACE.

# HE CREATED TIME FOR THE TEAM.

---

# 25. Why Modrić Was So Important to BBC

BBC was one of the most dangerous attacking units of the era.

But the front three depended on the team behind them.

Benzema could connect.

Bale could accelerate.

Cristiano could finish.

But someone had to move the ball from:

```text
RECOVERY
```

to:

```text
ATTACK.
```

Modrić was exceptionally good at that transition.

The relationship can be simplified as:

```text
MODRIĆ
ESCAPE PRESSURE
      ↓
KROOS
DISTRIBUTE
      ↓
BENZEMA
CONNECT
      ↓
RONALDO / BALE
ATTACK
```

Of course, real football was much more fluid than this.

But the principle remains.

# THE QUALITY OF THE FORWARD DEPENDS PARTLY ON THE QUALITY OF THE CONNECTION.

---

# 26. The Real Lesson of the Juventus Semi-Final

The Juventus tie is interesting precisely because Modrić did not play.

Sometimes a player's importance becomes clearer when he disappears.

With Modrić, Real Madrid possessed another solution against pressure:

```text
PRESS
  ↓
MODRIĆ
  ↓
TURN
  ↓
PROGRESS
```

Without him, Ancelotti had to find alternative solutions.

First leg:

# RAMOS IN MIDFIELD.

Second leg:

# KROOS + ISCO + JAMES.

Both approaches contained enormous quality.

Neither reproduced exactly what Modrić offered.

That tells us something important.

# A GREAT PLAYER IS NOT ALWAYS DIFFICULT TO REPLACE BECAUSE HE IS "BETTER."

# SOMETIMES HE IS DIFFICULT TO REPLACE BECAUSE HIS FUNCTION IS UNIQUE.

---

# 27. With Modrić vs Without Modrić

| Tactical Area       | With Modrić           | Without Modrić                   |
| ------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Press Resistance    | Turn / Escape / Carry | Greater reliance on passing      |
| Central Progression | Pass + Carry          | Fewer natural carrying solutions |
| Kroos' Role         | More specialised      | Greater responsibility           |
| Transition          | Quick connection      | Can become less dynamic          |
| Right-Side Support  | Natural rotations     | Different combinations required  |
| Midfield Mobility   | High                  | Risk of becoming more static     |
| Forward Supply      | Multiple routes       | Fewer progression mechanisms     |

This does not mean Real Madrid became a bad team without him.

Far from it.

It means:

# THE TEAM HAD FEWER SOLUTIONS.

At Champions League semi-final level, losing one important solution can matter enormously.

---

# 28. Modrić Was More Than a Playmaker

Calling Modrić a:

# PASSER

is too limited.

Calling him a:

# PLAYMAKER

is better.

But an even more useful tactical description is:

# PRESSURE-RESISTANT CONNECTOR.

Because he connected different phases of football.

```text
DEFENCE
   ↓
PRESSURE
   ↓
MODRIĆ
   ↓
ESCAPE
   ↓
MIDFIELD
   ↓
PROGRESSION
   ↓
ATTACK
```

He connected:

**Defence → Midfield**

**Midfield → Attack**

**Possession → Transition**

**Pressure → Space**

**Space → Time**

This is why his influence could spread across the entire team without always appearing in the final statistics.

---

# 29. The Most Important Question When Watching Modrić

Do not watch only the ball.

Watch what happens immediately before and after Modrić receives.

Ask:

**Did he scan before receiving?**

**Where is the pressure coming from?**

**Does he receive side-on?**

**Can he turn?**

**Does he attract another midfielder?**

**Does his carry force somebody out of position?**

**Does he release the ball before the defence recovers?**

Then watch the forwards.

```text
MODRIĆ TURNS
      ↓
MIDFIELDER ELIMINATED
      ↓
RONALDO STARTS RUN
      ↓
BALE ATTACKS SPACE
      ↓
BENZEMA MOVES BETWEEN LINES
```

Now Modrić's importance becomes much easier to see.

---

# Conclusion — Modrić Was the Bridge

Real Madrid's 2014/15 team contained extraordinary talent.

Cristiano Ronaldo could decide matches.

Gareth Bale provided explosive vertical speed.

Karim Benzema connected the attack.

James Rodríguez provided creativity.

Isco could manipulate tight spaces.

Toni Kroos controlled possession and distribution.

But Luka Modrić provided something particularly difficult to reproduce:

# CONNECTION THROUGH PRESSURE.

He could receive when the opponent pressed.

Turn.

Escape.

Carry.

Progress.

And then release the attacking talent ahead of him.

```text
DEFENCE
   ↓
PRESS
   ↓
MODRIĆ
   ↓
TURN
   ↓
ESCAPE
   ↓
PROGRESS
   ↓
BBC
   ↓
GOAL THREAT
```

That is why the 2015 Juventus semi-final is such an interesting Modrić case study.

It was not one of his greatest matches.

# HE DID NOT PLAY.

And that is precisely the point.

His absence exposed the functions Real Madrid normally received from him.

We cannot say Real Madrid would definitely have beaten Juventus with Modrić.

But we can say that without him, Real Madrid lost an elite combination of:

# PRESS RESISTANCE

# MOBILITY

# PROGRESSION

# TRANSITION CONNECTION

# MIDFIELD BALANCE

And those qualities were extremely difficult to replace.

So the best way to describe the 2014/15 midfield is not:

**Kroos and Modrić were two great passers.**

It is:

# KROOS COULD CONTROL THE GAME.

# MODRIĆ COULD MOVE THE GAME.

And together:

# THEY TURNED POSSESSION INTO ATTACK.

Cristiano and Bale gave Real Madrid extraordinary speed.

Benzema gave the front line intelligence and connection.

Kroos gave the midfield control.

And:

# MODRIĆ GAVE THE TEAM MOBILITY, PROGRESSION AND CONNECTION.

That is why Luka Modrić's importance cannot be understood only by watching his goals or assists.

Sometimes the clearest evidence of a midfielder's value appears when the ball can no longer reach the places it used to reach.

# GREAT ATTACKERS FINISH THE ATTACK.

# GREAT MIDFIELDERS MAKE THE ATTACK POSSIBLE.
