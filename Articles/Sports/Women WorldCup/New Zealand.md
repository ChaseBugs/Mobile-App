## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Five Games

### Game ① 2023 World Cup — New Zealand 1-0 Norway
- Wilkinson's goal; a disciplined block held Hegerberg's Norway.
**Lesson:** at their best, New Zealand can win a tight, low-scoring game.

### Game ② 29 October 2025 — USA 6-0 New Zealand
- Sears 8' — the USA scored three before half-time.
**Lesson:** **pace and pressing overwhelm them early.**

### Game ③ 23 & 26 October 2025 — Mexico 1-0 and 2-0
- Goals at 10', 11' and 16'.
**Lesson:** the same early collapse against a mid-level team.

### Game ④ 5 June 2026 — New Zealand 1-2 Haiti
- Bunge 41' → Haiti 45+3' and 47'.
**Lesson:** **concentration around half-time** is poor.

### Game ⑤ 9 June 2026 — New Zealand 0-0 Morocco
- A compact, physical game.
**Lesson:** against teams that don't press hard, the block can hold.

## 11. Weaknesses — With Evidence

1. **Almost no goals outside Oceania** — one in eight games.
2. **Early concessions** — 8', 10', 11', 13', 19' in five defeats.
3. **Around half-time** — Haiti scored 45+3' and 47'.
4. **Heavy defeats to fast, pressing teams** — USA 0-6, Australia 0-5.
5. **World Cup history** — never past the group stage; lost to the Philippines at home in 2023.
6. **Low-level club football** — few players in top leagues.

---

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Press high from the kick-off** — New Zealand concede in the first 20 minutes.
2. **Use pace in behind** — the USA and Australia scored freely.
3. **Attack at the end of the first half and the start of the second** — Haiti scored at 45+3' and 47'.
4. **Defend set pieces** — their main goal threat (Bunge, Stott).
5. **Keep the ball** — they cannot create without long balls and set pieces.

```text
HIGH PRESS IN THE FIRST 20 MINUTES
              +
PACE IN BEHIND
              +
PUSH AROUND HALF-TIME
              +
DEFEND SET PIECES
              ↓
NEW ZEALAND HAVE NO WAY BACK ONCE BEHIND
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

New Zealand are organised and physical, and they won Oceania qualifying without conceding. Against the rest of the world, the numbers are stark: **one goal in eight games, 19 conceded, and early goals almost every time.**

> **Start fast, run in behind, and New Zealand will not catch you.**
