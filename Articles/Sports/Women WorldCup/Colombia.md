# Colombia — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## Las Cafeteras

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Colombia play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

```text

HEAD COACH          Ángelo Marsiglia

CAPTAIN             Daniela Montoya (Alianza Lima, 35)
BEST WORLD CUP      Quarter-finals (2023)
QUALIFICATION       CONMEBOL Nations League 2025-26 champions — unbeaten, 20 points from 8 games (5 Jun 2026)
RECENT RESULTS      2025 Copa América final 4-4 (lost on pens to Brazil); Nations League champions
RECORD (12 MONTHS)  Unbeaten in South America; lost to Canada 1-4 and the USA 0-1 at the 2026 SheBelieves Cup
```

**One-line verdict:** One of the most exciting attacking sides at the tournament, built around **Linda Caicedo** (Real Madrid, pace 93) and **Mayra Ramírez** (Chelsea). They beat Germany at the 2023 World Cup and took Spain and Brazil to penalties. But they **concede a lot** (four in the Copa final, three to Paraguay), and they lose to physical North American teams.

## 5. Head Coach — Ángelo Marsiglia

- Led Colombia to the Copa América final and the inaugural CONMEBOL Nations League title.
- **Style (scouting previews):** wants to **turn defences and drop them back towards their own goal**; wide forwards **isolated 1v1**; full-backs push up when Colombia have sustained possession; **against stronger sides, more long balls over the midfield** into space.
- **Squad management:** Mayra Ramírez missed early 2026 after muscle surgery; **Catalina Usme (62 goals, record scorer) was left out** in 2026 — reportedly a technical decision.

## 6. Tactics and Formation

### 6.1 Shapes

```text
IN POSSESSION (4-4-2 / 4-2-3-1)

              RAMÍREZ             ROBLEDO / SANTOS
  CAICEDO (isolated 1v1)                       BONILLA / LANDÁZURY
            MONTOYA           RESTREPO / IZQUIERDO
  CARACAS     ARIAS      VANEGAS / CARABALÍ      GUZMÁN
```

```text
OUT OF POSSESSION vs STRONG TEAMS (4-5-1 → fast counter)

                    RAMÍREZ
  CAICEDO   SANTOS    MONTOYA    RESTREPO   BONILLA
  CARACAS    ARIAS    VANEGAS    GUZMÁN
                 PÉREZ
```

### 6.2 Isolating Caicedo 1v1
- Caicedo (EA FC 27: PAC 93, DRI 89) is moved to wherever Colombia can create a 1v1.
- **Copa final 25'** (after a sustained move), **88' assist** on the counter for Ramírez, 64' vs Argentina, 14' vs Chile.

### 6.3 Long Balls Into Space for Ramírez
- Ramírez (PAC 90, PHY 84) is a powerful, fast No.9 who runs in behind.

### 6.4 Leicy Santos — Set Pieces and Penalties
- **Free kick 115'** in the Copa final; **penalties** vs Peru (6'), Ecuador (45'), Canada (81'), Venezuela (22').

### 6.5 Late Goals
- Vanegas 90+7' header vs Germany (2023); Ramírez 88' and Santos 115' in the Copa final; Vanegas 90+6' pen vs Chile.

### 6.6 Game-State Behaviour

| State | What Colombia do |
|---|---|
| vs weaker teams | Keep the ball, push full-backs high |
| vs stronger teams | Deep block + long balls / counters |
| Leading | **Lose leads** — Brazil (Copa), Spain (2024), England (2023) |
| Trailing | Score late from set pieces and individual moments |

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Six Games Against Strong Opponents

### Game ① 30 July 2023, World Cup — Colombia 2-1 Germany
- Caicedo scored; Germany equalised late (Popp pen); **Vanegas headed the winner at 90+7'** from a corner.
**Lesson:** Colombia **score late from set pieces** and can beat a top European team.

### Game ② 12 August 2023, World Cup QF — England 2-1 Colombia
- **Santos 44'** gave Colombia the lead; England equalised in first-half stoppage time and won.
**Lesson:** Colombia **lose leads** to elite teams.

### Game ③ August 2024, Olympic QF — Spain 2-2 Colombia (Spain won on pens)
- Colombia led **2-0**; Spain came back and won the shootout.
**Lesson:** Same pattern — **brilliant start, cannot close it out.**

### Game ④ 3 August 2025, Copa Final — Brazil 4-4 Colombia (Brazil won 5-4 on pens)
| Minute | Moment |

**Lessons:** Colombia's **attack can score four against Brazil**, but they **conceded at 90+6'** and lost a shootout.

### Game ⑤ 1 & 7 March 2026, SheBelieves Cup — Canada 4-1 Colombia; USA 1-0 Colombia
- Conceded four to Canada; lost to an 82' USA goal.

### Game ⑥ 9 June 2026, Nations League — Paraguay 3-4 Colombia (Asunción)
- Three goals by half-time; then conceded at 43', 60' and 89'.
**Lesson:** Colombia **open games fast** and then **leak goals** even against a weaker side.

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Double up on Caicedo every time** — winger tracks, full-back stays tight, a midfielder covers inside. Never leave a 1v1.
2. **Defend deep enough to take away the space for Ramírez's runs.**
3. **Survive the first 30 minutes** — Colombia start fast (2-0 vs Spain; three by HT vs Paraguay).
4. **Then attack their defence physically** — Canada and the USA showed the way.
5. **Push late — Colombia concede late** (Marta 90+6', England's first-half added-time equaliser).
6. **Defend set pieces with our best headers** — Vanegas and Santos's free kicks.
7. **Take it to penalties** — Colombia have lost two big shootouts in a row.

```text
DOUBLE CAICEDO · DENY SPACE BEHIND FOR RAMÍREZ
                     +
SURVIVE THE FIRST 30 MINUTES
                     +
PHYSICAL PRESSURE ON A WEAK BACK LINE
                     +
LATE PUSH — COLOMBIA LOSE LEADS
                     ↓
LET COLOMBIA PLAY THEIR GAME FOR 30 MINUTES — THEN TAKE IT FROM THEM
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

Colombia have two of the most dangerous attackers in the world and a habit of taking big teams to the limit. But they lose leads, concede too many, and lose shootouts.

> **Stop Caicedo, stay in the game, and Colombia will give you chances at the end.**
