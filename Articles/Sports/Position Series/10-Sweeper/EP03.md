# EP03 — Der Kaiser

## How Did Beckenbauer Turn a Defender Into the Starting Point of the Attack?

17 June 1970, Mexico City, the Estadio Azteca. World Cup semi-final, Italy vs West Germany.

In the second half, Beckenbauer drove into the Italian box, went down, and dislocated his right shoulder.

West Germany had already used both of their substitutions.

Beckenbauer did not leave the pitch.

With his arm strapped to his body, he played on.

The game went to extra time at 1-1, and five goals were scored in extra time alone.

Italy won 4-3.

The match is still called **“the Game of the Century,”** and a plaque at the Azteca commemorates it.

And the image of Beckenbauer playing on with his arm strapped up became his symbol:

**“Der Kaiser” — the Emperor.**

---

# 1. From Midfielder to Libero

Beckenbauer was not a Libero from the start.

At the 1966 World Cup, he played for West Germany as **a midfielder.**

In the final against England, he was given the job of marking Bobby Charlton, and West Germany lost 4-2 after extra time.

Afterwards, at Bayern, he gradually dropped behind the defensive line.

And there, he realised something:

**The Libero has nobody to mark.**

**So when he has the ball, nobody is there to stop him.**

```text
A MIDFIELDER'S VISION + THE LIBERO'S FREEDOM
                   ↓
        BECKENBAUER'S LIBERO
```

# A MIDFIELDER'S MIND IN A DEFENDER'S POSITION

---

# 2. Libero 2.0 — The Libero Who Steps Forward

Beckenbauer's Libero was completely different from Picchi's.

```text
LIBERO 1.0 (PICCHI)              LIBERO 2.0 (BECKENBAUER)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STAYS BEHIND                     ADVANCES WITH THE BALL
CLEARS THE BALL                  STARTS ATTACKS WITH PASSES
THE DEFENCE'S LAST PLAYER        THE ATTACK'S FIRST PLAYER
```

When he received the ball behind the defensive line, he dribbled all the way into midfield.

Opponents were thrown.

**A player nobody had planned to mark was suddenly bearing down on them with the ball.**

```text
BECKENBAUER ADVANCES
          ↓
OPPOSING MIDFIELDER: DO I STOP HIM?
          ↓
IF HE DOES  → THE MAN HE WAS MARKING IS FREE
IF HE DOESN'T → BECKENBAUER KEEPS GOING
```

This is exactly the principle of **“Carry → Force a Decision”** that we saw for the modern centre-back in SERIES 07.

It already existed fifty years ago.

# THE FREE MAN WITH THE BALL

---

# 3. 1972 — European Champion and a First Ballon d'Or

In 1972, West Germany won the European Championship.

They beat the Soviet Union 3-0 in the final.

That side is regarded as one of the most beautiful in West Germany's history.

Beckenbauer played as Libero, while Günter Netzer directed the game from midfield.

That year, Beckenbauer won **his first Ballon d'Or.**

At the time, a defender winning the Ballon d'Or was extremely rare.

---

# 4. 1974 — Munich, Beating Cruyff

7 July 1974, Munich. World Cup final, West Germany vs the Netherlands.

The Netherlands were the team of Johan Cruyff and **Total Football.**

Straight from kick-off, Cruyff dribbled into the box and won a penalty.

The Netherlands led 1-0 before a West German player had even touched the ball.

But West Germany did not collapse.

Breitner's penalty equalised, and Gerd Müller scored the winner.

2-1. West Germany were world champions.

Their captain was Beckenbauer.

```text
CRUYFF — TOTAL FOOTBALL
            vs
BECKENBAUER — THE ADVANCING LIBERO
            ↓
      2-1 WEST GERMANY
```

That final was **a collision between the two ideas that changed football in the 1970s.**

# TWO REVOLUTIONS, ONE FINAL

---

# 5. Bayern — Three European Cups in a Row

At the same time, Bayern ruled Europe.

### 1974 — Brussels, Atlético Madrid

The first final finished 1-1 after extra time. It remains the only European Cup final ever to be **replayed.**

Two days later, Bayern won the replay **4-0**, with two goals each from Hoeneß and Müller.

### 1975 — Paris, Leeds United

Goals from Roth and Müller gave Bayern a **2-0** win.

### 1976 — Glasgow, Saint-Étienne

A Roth free kick won it **1-0.**

```text
1974  BAYERN 4-0 ATLÉTICO (REPLAY)
1975  BAYERN 2-0 LEEDS
1976  BAYERN 1-0 SAINT-ÉTIENNE
```

Beckenbauer, Gerd Müller, Sepp Maier, Paul Breitner, Uli Hoeneß.

This Bayern generation was also the core of the West German national team.

In 1976, Beckenbauer won **his second Ballon d'Or.**

He remains **the only defender** to have won the Ballon d'Or more than once.

# THREE IN A ROW

---

# 6. What Made Beckenbauer Special?

There were defenders who were good on the ball before Beckenbauer.

But Beckenbauer **changed what the Libero position itself meant.**

### 1. Vision

As a former midfielder, he saw the whole game.

### 2. Elegance

His dribbling and passing were closer to rhythm than to power. The pass with the outside of his foot was his signature.

### 3. Leadership

He directed his teammates on the pitch. That is why they called him “the Kaiser.”

### 4. Timing

He knew when to step forward and when to stay back.

```text
BECKENBAUER =
VISION + ELEGANCE + LEADERSHIP + TIMING
```

---

# 7. From Player to Coach — Rome, 1990

In 1977, Beckenbauer moved to the New York Cosmos in the United States.

Playing alongside Pelé, he helped drive America's football boom.

In 1984, he became coach of the West German national team.

In 1986, they reached the World Cup final and lost 3-2 to Argentina.

Then came **8 July 1990, Rome.** World Cup final, West Germany vs Argentina.

A Brehme penalty won it **1-0.**

West Germany were world champions.

Beckenbauer became one of only three people to win the World Cup as both a player and a coach.

The image of him walking slowly and alone across the pitch that night is still famous.

---

# 8. Beckenbauer's Legacy

In January 2024, Beckenbauer died at the age of 78.

What he left behind was not only trophies.

It was an idea: **a defender can dominate a game.**

```text
BECKENBAUER (1970s)      — THE ADVANCING LIBERO
          ↓
SAMMER (1990s)           — THE LAST GREAT LIBERO
          ↓
BALL-PLAYING CB (2010s)  — VAN DIJK, STONES
          ↓
HYBRID CB (2020s)        — STONES STEPPING INTO MIDFIELD
```

It is no exaggeration to say that the line of ball-playing defenders we followed in SERIES 07

all began with Beckenbauer.

# THE DEFENDER WHO RULED THE GAME

---

# Conclusion — The First Defender to Use His Freedom to Attack

Picchi used the Libero's freedom to **defend.**

Beckenbauer used it to **attack.**

That difference changed football.

> **Beckenbauer stood behind the defensive line. But he led the whole game from the front.**
