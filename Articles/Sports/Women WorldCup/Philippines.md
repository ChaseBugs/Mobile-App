# Philippines — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## Filipinas

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how the Philippines play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

RECORD vs TOP 20    L 6 — Australia 0-1, Korea 0-3, Japan 0-7 and 0-4, China 1-5 (and 2023 Norway 0-6)

**One-line verdict:** A hard-working, heavily diaspora-based team (US college, A-League, Scandinavia) that defends deep and well against mid-level sides — but **has been heavily beaten by every top Asian team** in 2026. Many players are **free agents or university students.**

## 5. Head Coach — Mark Torcaso

- Australian coach, appointed August 2023 after the World Cup.
- Met all his targets: SEA Games gold, Asian Cup QF, World Cup qualification ("Torcaso proud as Philippines meet all targets").
- **Pragmatic:** against Australia, his team "only appeared interested in limiting the damage in the first half" after losing 0-8 in 2023.
- **Flexible shape:** has switched between a **3-4-2-1** and a **4-4-2**.
- The team's "Aussie connection" (A-League clubs, Australian coaching) shaped the qualifying campaign.

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Five Games

### Game ① 2023 World Cup — Philippines 1-0 New Zealand
- Bolden's header; then defended a deep block. **Lesson:** they can win a low-scoring game against a mid-level team.

### Game ② 1 March 2026 — Australia 1-0 Philippines
- Kerr header 14'; the Philippines sat deep and limited damage.
**Lesson:** **Deep block keeps scores low — but they concede to headers.**

### Game ③ 5 March 2026 — Korea 3-0 Philippines
- Conceded at 12' and 15'.
**Lesson:** **Slow starts** against fast counter-attacking teams.

### Game ④ 15 March 2026 / 25 September 2026 — Japan 7-0, Japan 4-0
- Koga's two headers (set pieces); Japan's B-team scored four in the first half of the AG QF.
**Lesson:** Against technical teams, the block **collapses**.

### Game ⑤ 19 March 2026 — Uzbekistan 0-2 Philippines (play-in)
- **Beard 47', Sawicki 52'** — both assisted by Guy, right after half-time.
**Lesson:** The Philippines are **strongest at the start of the second half** against equal opponents.

## 11. Weaknesses — With Evidence

1. **Heavy defeats to top teams** — 0-7 and 0-4 (Japan), 1-5 (China), 0-3 (Korea), 0-6 (Norway 2023).
2. **Early goals against** — Korea 12', 15'; Japan AG 2'; Peru 1'.
3. **Set pieces and headers against** — Kerr header; Koga's two headers.
4. **Club situation** — several key players are free agents or in college football.
5. **Late concessions to equal teams** — Uzbekistan 67', 81' (2-2); Peru 86'.
6. **Low attacking output** — one goal in their four games against top-20 teams in 2026.

---

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Score early** — they concede in the first 15 minutes against good teams.
2. **Press their back three** — they are not comfortable building under pressure.
3. **Use set pieces and crosses** — headers beat them (Kerr, Koga).
4. **Be patient against the 5-4-1** — circulate, switch play, overload the wing-backs.
5. **Concentrate at the start of the second half** — their goals come there (47', 52').

```text
EARLY GOAL + HIGH PRESS ON THE BACK THREE
              +
CROSSES AND SET PIECES
              +
PATIENT CIRCULATION AGAINST THE 5-4-1
              ↓
THE PHILIPPINES CANNOT CHASE A GAME
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

The Philippines are well-organised, hard-working and capable of beating mid-level teams. Against top teams, they have been heavily beaten in 2026.

> **Score early and keep attacking — the Philippines' block cannot hold for 90 minutes against quality.**
