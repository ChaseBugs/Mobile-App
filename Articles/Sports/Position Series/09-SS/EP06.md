# SERIES 09 — The Second Striker

# EP06 — SPECIAL: The Greatest Strike Partnerships of the 21st Century

## Why the Best Attacking Partnership Is Not Simply Made of the Two Best Strikers

One great striker can change a match.

But historically, great attacks have rarely been built around **one** forward. They have been built around **two.**

The key is:

# PARTNERSHIP

When one drags a defender away, the other uses the space.

When one drops deep, the other runs in behind.

When one scores, the other has often created the goal.

That is why the formula for the greatest strike partnership is not:

# GREAT STRIKER + GREAT STRIKER

The real formula is:

# COMPLEMENTARY SKILLS + TIMING + TRUST + SPACE

So what were the greatest strike partnerships of the 21st century?

---

# The Criteria

**Complementarity** — how well their abilities complement each other

**Output** — goals and assists

**Trophies** — titles won together

**Influence** — the mark they left on football

# 1. Thierry Henry + Dennis Bergkamp

## Arsenal — Speed + Vision

```text
Complementarity  ★★★★★
Output           ★★★★★
Trophies         ★★★★★
Influence        ★★★★★
```

This is the ideal Second Striker partnership of this series.

**Henry** attacked the space behind the defence with his pace.

**Bergkamp** received the ball in the space in front of it and opened the path for Henry to run.

```text
      HENRY ───→ SPACE BEHIND THE DEFENCE
        ↑
   BERGKAMP'S PASS
 (BETWEEN THE LINES)
```

Together, they won the league and FA Cup double in 2001-02,

and the **unbeaten 2003-04 “Invincibles”** season.

That season, Henry was the league's top scorer with 30 goals — 41 per cent of Arsenal's league total.

Bergkamp scored four.

But many of Henry's goals began with Bergkamp's vision.

### May 2002, Old Trafford

Arsenal beat Manchester United 1-0 at Old Trafford to clinch the league title — completing the double at their rival's home.

### April 2004 — The Invincibles' Decisive Week

Against Liverpool, Arsenal were 2-1 down. A Henry hat-trick turned it into a 4-2 win.

A week later against Leeds, Henry scored **four.** Arsenal won 5-0.

# 2. Andriy Shevchenko + Filippo Inzaghi

## AC Milan — Complete + Poacher

```text
Complementarity  ★★★★★
Output           ★★★★
Trophies         ★★★★★
Influence        ★★★★
```

**Shevchenko** was a forward with everything: pace, shooting, dribbling and heading.

**Inzaghi** was the pure poacher we met in SERIES 01 — a player who lived on the offside line.

Shevchenko moved widely to build attacks, and Inzaghi finished them in the box.

May 2003, Old Trafford. Champions League final, Milan vs Juventus.

0-0 after extra time. Penalties.

The man who scored the decisive kick was Shevchenko.

Milan were champions of Europe.

### The 2003 Semi-Final — A Milan Derby

To reach that final, Milan had to get past Inter in the semi-final.

Both legs were played at the same San Siro.

The first leg ended 0-0, the second 1-1.

Shevchenko's goal in the second leg sent Milan through on away goals.

Four years later, in the 2007 final in Athens, Shevchenko had already left for Chelsea.

The player who scored twice against Liverpool to win the trophy for Milan was **Inzaghi.**

**The two halves of one partnership each decided a European final on their own.**

# 3. Luis Suárez + Daniel Sturridge

## Liverpool 2013-14 — Chaos + Finishing

```text
Complementarity  ★★★★★
Output           ★★★★★
Trophies         ★★
Influence        ★★★★
```

The pairing known as **“SAS”** earns its place on this list with a single season.

In 2013-14, Suárez was the league's top scorer with **31 goals.**

Sturridge scored **21.**

Together, they scored **52 league goals.**

Suárez created chaos with constant movement, dribbling and pressing.

Sturridge finished calmly in the middle of that chaos.

Liverpool finished second in the league.

They did not win the title, but the season is remembered as one of the most spectacular attacking campaigns in Premier League history.

### 5 May 2014 — “Crystanbul”

Selhurst Park. Crystal Palace vs Liverpool.

Liverpool led 3-0.

Needing goal difference in the title race, they kept attacking.

Then, in roughly the last ten minutes, they conceded three times. **3-3.**

After the final whistle, Suárez cried, hiding his face in his shirt.

Liverpool's title dream effectively ended that night.

**SAS were one of the most spectacular front twos in Premier League history — but that season also showed that attack alone does not win titles.**

# 4. Robert Lewandowski + Thomas Müller

## Bayern — Finisher + Raumdeuter

```text
Complementarity  ★★★★★
Output           ★★★★★
Trophies         ★★★★★
Influence        ★★★★
```

In 2019-20, Bayern won the treble.

Lewandowski scored **34 league goals.**

Müller provided **21 league assists** — a Bundesliga single-season record.

Lewandowski was the perfect finisher in the box.

Müller, the **Raumdeuter** we saw in EP04, found the empty spaces around Lewandowski and linked the play.

This partnership is the best example of **how the Second Striker survived in modern football.**

On paper, Müller was an attacking midfielder. In reality, he was Lewandowski's Second Striker.

### Five Goals in Nine Minutes

22 September 2015. Bayern vs Wolfsburg.

Bayern were 1-0 down at half-time.

Lewandowski came off the bench and scored **five goals in nine minutes.**

Bayern won 5-1.

### August 2020, Lisbon — 8-2

Champions League quarter-final, Bayern vs Barcelona.

Müller scored twice, Lewandowski once.

**One in the box, one in the space around it. That season, Bayern won the treble.**

# 5. Romelu Lukaku + Lautaro Martínez

## Inter 2020-21 — Power + Movement

```text
Complementarity  ★★★★★
Output           ★★★★
Trophies         ★★★★
Influence        ★★★★
```

In Conte's 3-5-2, **“LuLa”** led Inter to their first Serie A title in 11 years.

Lukaku scored 30 goals in all competitions and was named Serie A's Player of the Year.

Lautaro scored 19 in all competitions.

Lukaku's power pushed defenders back, and Lautaro attacked the space around him.

The defining night came in the February 2021 derby: Lautaro scored twice, Lukaku once, and Inter beat Milan 3-0 on the way to the title.

This pairing proved that **a modern front two can win titles.**

# 6. Marcus Thuram + Lautaro Martínez

## Inter 2023 Onwards — The Modern Front Two

```text
Complementarity  ★★★★★
Output           ★★★★
Trophies         ★★★★★
Influence        ★★★★★
```

**“ThuLa”** is the model of the modern front two we saw in EP05.

The 2023-24 Serie A title, with Lautaro as the league's top scorer.

A place in the 2025 Champions League final.

And another Serie A title in 2025-26.

The defining feature of this pair is **role rotation.**

Who is the first striker and who is the second is never fixed.

And both of them press.

Their story is told in Milan derbies: the 5-1 in September 2023, when Thuram curled in a stunning goal in his first derby, and the 2-1 in April 2024, when Thuram scored again as Inter sealed the title at their rival's home.

# THE FRONT TWO, REINVENTED

The front two of the **impossible title** we saw in EP03 — and the beginning of the modern 4-4-2 in which both forwards press.

Alongside Neymar, “MSN” was a trio, but the relationship between Messi and Suárez showed how destructive a False 9 and a No.9 can be together. They won the treble in 2015.

# 9. What Great Partnerships Have in Common

These six partnerships share several things.

### 1. Different Abilities

Speed + vision, power + movement, finishing + reading space. Almost none of them paired two players of the same type.

### 2. Shared Space

When one created space, the other used it.

### 3. Timing

The moment of the pass and the moment of the run matched.

### 4. Selflessness

Even when one took the goalscoring records, the other kept moving to make them possible. Bergkamp, Müller and Firmino all did this.

```text
GREAT PARTNERSHIP =
DIFFERENT SKILLS + SHARED SPACE + TIMING + SACRIFICE
```

# 10. So Who Is the Greatest?

Let us recall the conclusion of this series:

**The Second Striker is not a position. He is a relationship.**

By that standard, the greatest strike partnership of the 21st century is:

# HENRY + BERGKAMP

One ran with the greatest pace in the world.

The other opened the way with the greatest vision in the world.

And together they created one of the most special records in football history: an unbeaten league season.

**The most modern partnership** is Thuram + Lautaro.

**The most complete partnership** is Lewandowski + Müller.

**The most explosive single season** belongs to Suárez + Sturridge.

**The most hidden Second Striker** was Müller.
