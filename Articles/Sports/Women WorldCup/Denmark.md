# Denmark — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## De Rød-Hvide

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Denmark play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

## 0. At a Glance

```text
FIFA RANKING        12th (official release of 16 June 2026) — highest-ever 6th (2007, 2009)
HEAD COACH          Jakob Michelsen (since 1 August 2025)
BASE FORMATION      4-3-3 / 4-2-3-1 — compact, counter-attacking against stronger teams
CAPTAIN             Pernille Harder (FC Bayern) — record scorer, 87 goals
BEST WORLD CUP      Quarter-finals (1991, 1995); 2023: round of 16
QUALIFICATION       UEFA League A Group 1 winners (9 June 2026) — ahead of Sweden and Italy
RECENT RESULTS      Euro 2025: three defeats; since Michelsen: unbeaten in 11 (W 7, D 4)
```

**One-line verdict:** A team transformed by Michelsen — from three straight Euro 2025 defeats to an **11-game unbeaten run**, including **two wins over Sweden** and a 4-3 win in Canada. They defend deep, counter fast, and rely heavily on **Pernille Harder**. Without her — or when pinned back for long spells — they are vulnerable.

### Profile

```text
OUT OF POSSESSION (LOW 4-5-1 / 4-4-2 vs STRONG TEAMS)

  THOMSEN    KÜHL      SNERLE      FLØE    HOLDT
  THRIGE / SVAVA  BALLISAGER   BOYE   THØGERSEN
```

```text
IN POSSESSION (4-2-3-1 → quick transitions)

                    VANGSGAARD
  THOMSEN         HARDER          HOLDT
```

### 6.2 Counter-Attacks

### 6.4 Long Shots and Set Plays

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Shut down Harder.** Man-mark her when she drops deep; a second player screens the passing lane. If she starts on the bench, **prepare for her 60' entry.**
2. **Keep the ball — but keep rest-defence.** Denmark sit deep and counter: always leave two defenders and a midfielder behind the ball (Thomsen's counter in Gothenburg).
3. **Pace in behind Ballisager** (PAC 47).
4. **Push late — Denmark concede late.** Canada scored at ~70' and with a late sub; Germany came back at Euro 2025.
5. **Use crosses into a crowded box.** Canada's 10' header came from a cross into a packed area.
6. **Make them attack us.** Against Italy's deep block, Denmark scored once in 180 minutes.

```text
NEUTRALISE HARDER (STARTER OR SUB)
              +
POSSESSION WITH STRONG REST-DEFENCE
              +
PACE IN BEHIND BALLISAGER
              +
LATE PRESSURE — FRESH ATTACKERS AFTER 60'
              ↓
DENMARK WITHOUT HARDER'S MOMENTS HAVE LITTLE ELSE
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

Denmark have been transformed under Michelsen: unbeaten in 11, group winners above Sweden and Italy, comfortable defending deep and countering. But:

1. **They depend on Pernille Harder**
2. **They struggle to protect leads**
3. **Their centre-backs are slow**
4. **They create little against disciplined defences**

> **Take Harder out of the game, keep your shape behind the ball, and Denmark run out of answers.**
