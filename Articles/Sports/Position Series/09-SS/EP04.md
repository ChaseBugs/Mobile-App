# SERIES 09 — The Second Striker

# EP04 — Reincarnation: False 9, Raumdeuter, Inside Forward

## Where Did the Second Striker's Role Go?

2 May 2009, the Bernabéu. Real Madrid vs Barcelona.

About ten minutes into the game,

Guardiola gave a signal from the bench.

**Lionel Messi**, who had started on the right, moved into the centre.

Samuel Eto'o, who had started as the centre-forward, moved out wide.

But Messi did not stand between the centre-backs like a classic striker.

He kept dropping **deeper.**

Real Madrid's centre-backs could not decide whether to follow him or hold their position.

Barcelona won 6-2.

Xavi later said:

> **“That was the game that he played Messi as the false nine.”**

The role Totti had shown at Roma was being perfected on the biggest stage in the world.

**The Second Striker had not disappeared. He had been reborn.**

---

# 1. The Second Striker's DNA

First, let us set out the Second Striker's core abilities.

```text
SECOND STRIKER DNA

1. RECEIVES THE BALL BETWEEN THE LINES
2. TURNS TO FACE GOAL
3. SCORES GOALS
4. CREATES GOALS
5. CREATES SPACE FOR HIS STRIKE PARTNER
```

In modern football, these five abilities did not survive in a single position.

They survived **split across several roles.**

```text
                    SECOND STRIKER DNA
                           │
   ┌───────────┬───────────┼────────────┬─────────────┐
   ↓           ↓           ↓            ↓             ↓
FALSE 9   RAUMDEUTER  DEEP-LYING 9  INSIDE FWD  ATTACKING No.8/10
```

---

# 2. The False 9 — The Forward Who Drops Deep

### Totti (2005-07)

At Spalletti's Roma, Totti started up front but kept dropping deep to receive the ball.

Roma won 11 Serie A games in a row, and in 2006-07 Totti scored 26 goals to win the European Golden Shoe.

### Messi (2009 onwards)

At Guardiola's Barcelona, Messi became the complete False 9.

In 2011-12, Messi scored **73 goals** in all competitions, including 50 in La Liga.

### Two Finals

May 2009, Rome. Champions League final, Barcelona vs Manchester United.

Messi played as a False 9 and scored a second-half header. Barcelona won 2-0.

May 2011, Wembley. The same two teams in the final.

Messi played as a False 9 again and scored, and Barcelona won 3-1.

**In both finals, United's centre-backs never solved the question of whether to follow Messi or stay.**

### The Principle of the False 9

```text
THE FALSE 9 DROPS DEEP
          ↓
THE OPPOSING CB'S DILEMMA
          ↓
IF HE FOLLOWS → SPACE BEHIND → WINGERS / MIDFIELDERS RUN IN
IF HE STAYS   → THE FALSE 9 IS FREE → THE ATTACK BUILDS
```

The False 9 is **the Second Striker moved up to the front line.**

One lone striker does the job that used to belong to the “second forward” of a front two.

# THE SECOND STRIKER WITHOUT A FIRST STRIKER

---

# 3. The Raumdeuter — The Interpreter of Space

**Thomas Müller** was one of the most unusual attackers in modern football.

He was not quick.

His dribbling was not spectacular.

His shooting was not especially powerful.

But he always arrived **first in the space where the goal would come from.**

Müller described himself with one word:

# RAUMDEUTER

**“The interpreter of space.”**

```text
THE OPPONENT'S DEFENCE SHIFTS
             ↓
MÜLLER PREDICTS THE EMPTY SPACE
             ↓
ARRIVES BEFORE ANYONE ELSE
             ↓
GOAL OR ASSIST
```

In 2019-20, Müller set a Bundesliga record with **21 assists** in a single season.

That season, Lewandowski scored 34 league goals, and Bayern won the treble.

Müller moved beside and behind Lewandowski.

**He was, in effect, a 21st-century Second Striker.**

### Müller's Big Stages

At the 2010 World Cup in South Africa, the 20-year-old Müller scored five goals and made three assists, winning the **Golden Boot** and the Best Young Player award.

8 July 2014, Belo Horizonte. World Cup semi-final, Brazil vs Germany.

In the 11th minute, Müller drifted into the one unmarked space at a corner and scored the opener.

Germany won **7-1.**

August 2020, Lisbon. Champions League quarter-final, Bayern vs Barcelona.

Müller scored twice, and Lewandowski scored too. Bayern won **8-2.**

**Müller's goals were rarely spectacular. But where he stood was always right.**

In 2025, Müller left Bayern for the Vancouver Whitecaps in MLS.

# INTELLIGENCE IS THE FASTEST ABILITY

---

# 4. The No.9 Who Connects — Benzema

**Karim Benzema** spent much of his career playing for other people.

In nine seasons alongside Cristiano Ronaldo at Real Madrid, Benzema dropped deep from the front line, linked play and created space for Ronaldo.

On paper, he was the No.9. In practice, his role was much closer to **a Second Striker.**

```text
2009-18                      2018-22
BENZEMA = THE CONNECTOR       BENZEMA = THE STAR
RONALDO = THE SCORER
```

After Ronaldo left in 2018, Benzema became the centre of the team.

### 9 March 2022, the Bernabéu

Champions League round of 16, second leg. Real Madrid vs PSG.

Real Madrid trailed 2-0 on aggregate.

In the 61st minute, Benzema pounced on a Donnarumma mistake to score.

In the 76th minute, he scored again.

In the 78th minute, again.

**A hat-trick in around 17 minutes.** Real Madrid won 3-2 on aggregate.

At 34 years and 80 days, Benzema became the oldest player to score a Champions League hat-trick.

That season, he led Real Madrid to La Liga and the Champions League and won the **2022 Ballon d'Or.**

**A player who had spent nine years as the connector had become the star.**

Benzema showed how dangerous a player with a Second Striker's abilities can be once he becomes the lone striker.

# THE CONNECTOR BECOMES THE STAR

---

# 5. The Deep-Lying 9 — Kane

**Harry Kane** looks like a classic striker.

But he often drops into midfield to receive the ball, then plays long passes to teammates running in from the flanks or from behind.

```text
KANE DROPS DEEP → RECEIVES THE BALL
           ↓
A TEAMMATE RUNS IN BEHIND
           ↓
KANE'S LONG PASS
           ↓
CHANCE
```

The No.9 pins the centre-backs. The Second Striker drops into the space in front of them.

Kane does both.

And at Bayern in 2025-26, he scored **36 Bundesliga goals**, finishing as the league's top scorer for the third year in a row and winning the European Golden Shoe.

Kane carries **the No.9 and the Second Striker in one body.**

# STRIKER AND SECOND STRIKER IN ONE

---

# 6. The Inside Forward — The Goalscorer Who Moved Wide

Part of the Second Striker's goal threat moved **out wide.**

As we saw in SERIES 05, the modern winger exists to score rather than to cross.

In 2017-18, **Mohamed Salah** cut inside from the right to score **32 league goals.**

At the time, it was the record for a 38-game Premier League season. Haaland broke it with 36 in 2022-23.

Liverpool's attacking structure was built around Firmino.

When Firmino dropped deep as a False 9, Salah and Mané attacked inside from the flanks.

```text
     SALAH          MANÉ
        ↘          ↙
          FIRMINO
        (DROPS DEEP)
```

Firmino did not score many goals himself, but his movement created goals for Salah and Mané.

**Firmino took the Second Striker's “creating space,” and Salah took his “scoring goals.”**

# THE ROLE SPLIT INTO PIECES

---

# 7. The Attacking Midfielder — Bellingham

Another type of player with Second Striker DNA is **the attacking midfielder.**

In 2023-24, **Jude Bellingham** played in midfield for Real Madrid but kept arriving in the box, scoring **19 La Liga goals.**

Real Madrid won both La Liga and the Champions League that season.

### Two Clásicos

October 2023, Montjuïc. Bellingham scored twice against Barcelona to lead Real Madrid to a 2-1 comeback win.

April 2024, the Bernabéu. At 2-2, in stoppage time, Bellingham scored the winner. Real Madrid won 3-2.

**The timing of a midfielder arriving in the box — that is what made both Gerrard and Bellingham Second Strikers.**

Bellingham was the modern version of the Gerrard we saw in EP03.

**A midfielder had taken over the Second Striker's space.**

---

# 8. Griezmann — The Closest Heir

Among all these roles, the player closest to the classic Second Striker was **Antoine Griezmann.**

In Simeone's 4-4-2, playing alongside the centre-forward, Griezmann:

**dropped deep to receive the ball,**

**linked play with his teammates,**

**scored goals himself,**

**and defended.**

He became Atlético Madrid's all-time top scorer with 211 goals and led France to the 2018 World Cup.

When he moved to Orlando City in 2026, European football lost one of the last players closest to the classic Second Striker.

# ONE ROLE, MANY HEIRS

---

# Conclusion — One Position Split Into Many Roles

The Second Striker was one position.

But in modern football, his abilities were divided among several roles.

**The ability to drop deep** went to the False 9.

**The ability to read space** went to the Raumdeuter.

**The ability to connect** went to the deep-lying 9.

**The ability to score** went to the inside forward.

**The ability to arrive in the box** went to the attacking midfielder.

> **The Second Striker never disappeared. He was split into five roles and scattered across the whole pitch.**
