# Korea Republic — 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup Opponent Analysis

## Taeguk Ladies

> Data as of 17 October 2026. The 2027 Women's World Cup will be held in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. The final tournament draw has not yet taken place.
>
> **Purpose:** a scouting report for coaching staff — how Korea Republic play, who decides their games, where they are vulnerable, and a concrete game plan.

---

## 0. At a Glance

```text
FIFA RANKING        19th (official release of 16 June 2026) — highest-ever 14th
HEAD COACH          Shin Sang-woo (since mid-2025, after Colin Bell left by "mutual agreement")
BASE FORMATION      4-4-2 (front two); has experimented with several systems
CAPTAIN             Ko Yoo-jin (Incheon Hyundai Steel) — Lee Young-ju captained earlier under Shin
BEST WORLD CUP      Round of 16 (2015)
QUALIFICATION       2026 Asian Cup QF winners (14 March 2026, 6-0 vs Uzbekistan)
RECENT RESULTS      2026 Asian Cup SF (1-4 vs Japan); 2026 Asian Games bronze (1-0 vs China); EAFF E-1 2025 champions
RECORD (12 MONTHS)  Strong vs Asia's lower/middle tier; heavy defeats vs elite non-Asian teams (NED 0-5, BRA 1-5, CAN 1-3)
```

**One-line verdict:** A hard-working, counter-attacking 4-4-2 side with an ageing core (Ji So-yun 35, Kim Hye-ri 36, Jang Sel-gi 32) and a fast, direct front two. They held Australia 3-3 and beat China — but **they concede heavily against elite opponents** and struggle to close out games.

## 10. Signature Tactics — Analysis of Seven Games Against Strong Opponents

### Game ① 2023 World Cup — Korea Republic 1-1 Germany
- Held the two-time champions; knocked Germany out of the World Cup.
**Lesson:** Korea can **frustrate a top team** with a compact block.

### Game ② 2 December 2025 — Netherlands 5-0 Korea Republic
- **Miedema 9', 17', 31', 38'**; Peddemors 43' — five by half-time.
**Lesson:** Against a world-class striker, Korea's back line **collapsed.**

### Game ④ 18 March 2026, Asian Cup SF — Japan 4-1 Korea Republic
- Ueki 15', Hamano 25' (dribbled past two defenders), Kumagai 75' (corner header), Chiba 81'; Kang Chae-rim 78'.
**Lesson:** Against a technical, quick-passing team, **Korea's midfield is overrun.**

### Game ⑤ 11 & 14 April 2026 — Brazil 5-1 Korea; Canada 3-1 Korea
- Conceded eight in two games.
**Lesson:** **Physical, fast teams overwhelm Korea.**

### Game ⑥ 21 September 2026, Asian Games — DPRK 1-1 Korea Republic
- DPRK 35' from a Jon Ryong-jong corner; **Yoon Su-jeong 46'** straight after half-time.
**Lesson:** Korea's domestic squad held the DPRK — **strong reaction after half-time.**

### Game ⑦ 2 October 2026, Asian Games Bronze — Korea Republic 1-0 China
- **Noh Jin-young header 4'** (free kick, Choe Yu-ri pass); held China without a first-half shot on target; GK save at 50'.
**Lesson:** **An early set-piece goal + disciplined block** — Korea's best route to winning.

## 12. How to Beat This Team — Game Plan

1. **Press high and run at the back line early.** The Netherlands scored four by 38', Brazil five. Korea's defenders struggle against pace and movement.
2. **Overload the midfield and keep the ball.** Japan's possession overran Korea twice. Make Ji So-yun (35) defend for 90 minutes.
3. **Win set pieces and attack scrambles.** Australia scored twice from box scrambles; Japan from a corner.
4. **Don't over-commit — Korea counter fast.** Keep two players behind the ball; watch Jeon Yu-gyeong's crosses and Mun Eun-ju's runs.
5. **Keep pushing late.** Korea concede late (90+8') and when they defend leads.
6. **Prepare for their bench at 50–60'.** Kim Shin-ji and Kang Chae-rim changed the Australia game.

```text
HIGH PRESS + PACE AT THE BACK LINE
              +
MIDFIELD OVERLOAD — MAKE JI SO-YUN RUN
              +
SET PIECES AND BOX PRESSURE
              +
REST DEFENCE AGAINST THE COUNTER
              ↓
KOREA CANNOT LIVE WITH ELITE INTENSITY FOR 90 MINUTES
```

---

## 13. Conclusion

Korea are organised, fast on the counter and dangerous from the bench, with real experience in Ji So-yun. Against Asia's middle tier they win comfortably.

But against elite opponents the evidence is overwhelming:

1. **Heavy defeats** (0-5, 1-5, 1-4)
2. **Set pieces and box scrambles against**
3. **An ageing spine**
4. **Difficulty closing out games**

> **Respect Korea's counter — then press them, out-run them and out-last them.**
